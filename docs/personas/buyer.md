# The platform for the buyer

The persona everything else exists to serve. Written from the outside in: what you see, what you get,
and what is actually being promised.

## What this is

You want to start a business and you do not know which idea is worth a year of your life.

This platform generates candidate ideas, then puts each one through seven checks against sources it
actually goes and reads. Most ideas die. You are shown why they died, with citations. The survivors
get a research pack, and that is what is for sale.

**The product is not the idea. It is the evidence, and the fact that most ideas failed.** Anyone can
sell you a list of business ideas. The interesting artefact here is the kill log.

## The seven checks

Six of them can end an idea:

| Check | The question |
|---|---|
| `pain_reality` | Does the problem actually hurt anyone |
| `value_durability` | Will this still be worth something in a few years |
| `incumbency` | Is somebody already doing this well |
| `payer_solvency` | Can the people with this problem afford to pay |
| `distribution` | Can you reach them |
| `legality` | Is it lawful |

The seventh, `price_comparables`, gathers what buyers already pay for similar things. **It can never
kill an idea**, because "no price page exists on the open web" is a fact about the web, not about
your idea.

An idea is only killed when there is **cited evidence against it.** Silence — nothing found either
way — never kills. It is recorded as `unverifiable` and the idea moves on.

## The promise, stated exactly

- **Every factual claim and every number cites a source you can open**, or is marked `unverifiable`.
  No unsourced numbers ship.
- **Verdicts are made only from passages actually fetched.** Not from what a model happens to
  remember.
- **Sources are archived when the check is run**, so if a page disappears later, the passage that
  supported the claim still exists.
- **Ideas are graded against their own class.** A £30 side hustle is not judged against the bar for a
  venture-scale company. Four ambition tiers run at once and each idea is judged in its own.

## Buying

1. Browse `mumchimp.com`.
2. Open a pack.
3. Pay through Stripe. Card details go to Stripe; this platform never sees them.
4. Your pack is delivered immediately.

**There is no account.** No password to create, no login to remember, nothing to be breached. Access
is a link with a token in it.

The trade-off is real and you should know it: **that link is your key.** Keep it. Anyone who has it
can download the pack, and there is currently no self-service way to look it up again — that means
emailing support.

Prices are set in your currency by market. US buyers are billed in USD.

## What this will not do for you

Said plainly, because an honest limit is worth more than a claim:

- It does not predict whether *you* will succeed. It grades the idea, not the operator.
- It is not investment or financial advice.
- It cannot see anything that is not published on the open web. A market with no public evidence
  reads as `unverifiable`, not as an opportunity.
- The catalogue is small on purpose. Most generated ideas do not survive the checks, and the gates are
  not loosened to fill shelves.

## Questions worth asking before you buy

**"How do I know the checks actually ran?"** Every pack carries its dossier: each check, its verdict,
its confidence, and the sources. If a check could not be answered it says so rather than guessing.

**"Why is the catalogue so small?"** Because the pass rate is genuinely low. Across 221 graded ideas,
the best-performing tier passed 11.8% and the venture tier passed none. Filling the catalogue would
mean lowering the bar, which would make every pack worth less.

**"What if a cited source is now gone?"** The passage was archived at the time the check ran.
