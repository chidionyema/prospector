The Probate Property Clear-Out Service.

A solo-operated service for primary carers who inherit a deceased relative's home: you handle the entire contents clearance, sorting, donation, sale, and rubbish removal, taking a fixed percentage of net proceeds from sold items plus a flat clearance fee.

Primary carers (especially those caring for elderly parents) often inherit cluttered homes they cannot physically or emotionally clear. Estate agents, house clearance firms, and auction houses each own one step but none will coordinate the whole messy chain for a single point-of-contact, percentage-based payout. By inverting the 'I am selling my stuff' model to 'someone else manages the entire exit for a cut', you create a low-capital, high-trust service that only works when the carer is time-poor and overwhelmed.
