ADIRenewDesk — The sole-trader driving instructor's DVSA registration renewal and standards-check evidence pack.

A fixed-fee service that compiles, checks and submits your Approved Driving Instructor registration renewal before the expiry deadline.

Sole‑trader driving instructors must renew their ADI registration with the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA) every four years. The process demands a completed application form, an enhanced DBS check, evidence of having passed a standards check (or declaration of exemption), and sometimes a medical report. Instructors find the paperwork confusing and risk losing their livelihood if a renewal lapses.

This service works like PitchBrief – the proven licence‑renewal desk for street traders – but transplanted to the driving‑instruction sector. A solo operator (leveraging AI to scan documents and pre‑fill forms) acts as a transaction broker, taking the instructor’s raw documents and delivering a submission‑ready pack, a tracked DVSA receipt, and a reminder for the next renewal. It operates on a flat fee per renewal (e.g. £149), which is a fraction of the lost income from a suspension.

The value capture sits in a proprietary database of DVSA local‑office nuances, common error‑triggers, and DBS processing hacks gathered with each application. A generic accountant or virtual assistant cannot match that depth on a low‑volume task, and the DVSA’s free guidance does not replace a done‑for‑you check.
