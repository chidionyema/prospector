HeirloomMatch — The Carer-Executor's Specialist Estate Match-Sale Desk.

A per-transaction broker that ingests a deceased carer's relative's specialist hobby collection (stamps, model railways, fountain pens, vinyl, militaria, vintage cameras, classic watches, scientific instruments), routes each category to a curated panel of vetted specialist collector-dealers via sealed-bid mini-auctions, and takes 15% on the cleared total.

When a primary carer becomes executor, the bulk of estate value from hobby collections is squandered on house-clearance firms paying 5–10%, dumped at charity shops, or left on the kerb because specialist collector-dealers — who would actually pay 40–60% of true auction value for the right category — will not engage with one-off, time-poor, distant private sellers. A solo broker who curates a vetted, paying panel of specialist dealers per category and routes estate lots via category-keyed sealed-bid matching salvages dramatically more value than generalist auction houses (who charge 25–30% and refuse sub-£500 lots) or self-serve eBay/Facebook-collector-group listings (which leave category-specific pricing on the table because executors don't know the specialists).
