The hypothesis that a niche micro-SaaS for school outdoor education can thrive on discretionary departmental budget is a deliverability failure. UK schools operate under strict procurement and data compliance regulations (e.g., GDPR, DfE standards) that prevent the adoption of 'operational hubs' via discretionary spend. The idea fails to acknowledge that schools cannot adopt new software without enterprise-level procurement, integration, and security clearance. As an info-product, it fails to solve the actual pain point, which is not 'workflow management' but 'regulatory compliance and procurement approval'.
