FlexCarer Job Broker.

A reverse‑recruitment transaction broker matching primary carers with genuinely flexible, remote part‑time jobs, charging the carer a one‑time placement fee only after they start earning—powered by a proprietary employer‑flexibility database and direct negotiation.

Mainstream job boards lack nuance on real‑time flexibility (e.g., split shifts, last‑minute cancellations due to care crises). A solo broker can build a hidden‑market intelligence layer by surveying employers already known for accommodating carers, negotiating custom arrangements, and placing carers into roles they would never find alone. The carer pays a flat fee of one week's salary upon placement, aligning incentives.

The primary carer pays a one‑time fee equivalent to one week's gross pay, due after they work their first month.

Post‑pandemic remote work has exploded, but companies still advertise 'flexible' without meaning it; carers need a trusted intermediary who can verify and enforce genuine flexibility. The rise of fractional and project‑based work creates openings a human‑curated broker can exploit.

Multiple passages confirm a real, underserved market for caregiver-specific flexible job matching (Groundwork launched to address this) and evidence that people pay for reverse recruiting services, indicating the value is real and not yet fully commoditised.

Groundwork and HireMamas are both incumbents already connecting caregivers (including moms and dads) with part‑time, flexible, and remote work, directly addressing the same need.
