HopAlpha – The Nano-Brewery Hop Contract Optimizer.

A mobile-first web app that helps nano-breweries (1-5 barrel) manage forward hop contracts, time spot purchases, and forecast alpha acid degradation to slash raw material waste and lock in lower prices.
