The Relocation Dislocation Top‑Up.

A mutual guarantee that pays civil‑service relocators the shortfall between their employer’s moving allowance and actual hard costs, using a proprietary benchmark of unreimbursed expenses gathered from FOI‑surveyed employees.

When civil servants (or NHS managers) are posted to a new region, the official relocation package often falls far short of real‑world double‑rent, stamp duty, or removal costs. A solo‑run mutual can capture this gap risk by collecting small premiums from relocating workers against a parametric trigger (firm posting order), paying out a predetermined sum scaled to the destination cost‑of‑relocation index the solo calculates.
