TankShield – The Independent Driver's Diesel Price Floor.

A solo-run service that offers small-scale truck and van drivers a fuel price ceiling: pay a monthly fee, and if the average diesel price in your region exceeds a pre-set cap, you get the difference paid.

Taking inspiration from airline fuel hedging (cross-sector: aviation finance), this product atomizes fuel price risk for the 1-5 vehicle operator using a pooled risk model and AI-driven price volatility forecasting.

Owner-operator truck drivers, courier van fleets with fewer than 5 vehicles, and tradespeople who drive extensively (e.g., plumbers, electricians).

Extreme fuel price spikes due to geopolitical instability, carbon taxes, and supply uncertainty; drivers are price-takers and cannot raise customer rates quickly enough.
