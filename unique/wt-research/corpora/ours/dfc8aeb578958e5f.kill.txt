NightBay, a safe walk to reserved parking for night shifts.

A local round near big hospitals that reserves you the same driveway parking space every night shift, and walks you to your car in the dark.

One person leases a small block of driveways within a five-minute walk of a hospital's staff entrance, from homeowners on the surrounding streets. Night-shift staff pay a monthly fee for a guaranteed space in that block, matched to their rota, and the operator personally walks them between the hospital door and their car at shift changeover, since that is when campus car parks feel most exposed.

Many NHS trusts brought back staff parking charges after the pandemic-era free period ended, and staff permits are often capped or allocated by seniority, so night-shift bank and agency staff can end up without a guaranteed space near the door. Trusts also run informal 'safe walk' buddy schemes because lone-working safety on a dark, near-empty campus is a recognised concern, but these rely on volunteers and rarely cover every shift or every worker.

This borrows the way airport valet firms guarantee you the same reserved bay and walk you to the terminal door, and applies it to a hospital car park at 2am. A national driveway-listing app can find a space for one night, but it cannot promise the same space every Tuesday and Thursday to match a rotating rota, and it will not send a person to walk anyone to their car.

The driveways in the block are leased directly and exclusively by the operator, not listed on any open app, so a marketplace cannot simply undercut by listing the same spaces. Once a worker's rota, their driveway and the walk routine are all set, dropping the service means losing both the guaranteed space and the escort, so there is little reason to switch to a one-off booking app instead.
