ToolGuard — we run your broken-tool warranty claim so you get a replacement fast, and you never fill in a form.

A fixed-fee warranty claim service for self-employed tradespeople: we handle the whole process with the manufacturer, so you get your tool back or replaced within 48 hours.

When a cordless drill or angle grinder stops mid-job, a self-employed electrician or plumber loses income with every hour the tool is dead. Most power tools carry a manufacturer’s warranty, but claiming it means digging out receipts, reading serial numbers, waiting on hold, and often settling for a repair that takes weeks. This service acts like the corporate fleet department that handles vehicle repairs for a haulier, but for the tools a sole trader carries in her van. The tradesperson texts a photo of the broken tool to us. We pull up its warranty registration, call the manufacturer, push for the fastest resolution (often advance replacement under the Consumer Rights Act), and arrange collection and delivery. We charge £9 a month for unlimited claims on tools we have on file, or £30 per one-off claim. The real value is speed: we know which brands replace within 24 hours and which argue, so we can escalate accordingly. We also build a dataset of tool failure rates, which we can later sell back to tool buyers as buying advice.

Self-employed electricians, plumbers, carpenters, and builders in the UK who rely on power tools daily and can't afford a day without them. They pay by direct debit or card, typically from a business bank account.

Tool warranties are getting longer (some brands now offer 3–5 years) but the claims process remains a call centre designed to discourage claims. At the same time, tradespeople have become more aware of their consumer rights post-Brexit via plain-English campaigns like Which?. There is no company handling the claim on their behalf as a service, while companies like AutoProtect handle car warranty claims for drivers. The gap exists because no one has yet packaged this for the self-employed tradesperson.
