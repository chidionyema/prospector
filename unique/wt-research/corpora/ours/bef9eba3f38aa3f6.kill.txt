RentBump Fight – the fixed-fee service that challenges an unfair rent increase at the tribunal.

A flat-fee service preparing your case to the First-tier Tribunal to set a fair market rent, backed by local rental data.

A landlord sends a Section 13 notice proposing a sky-high rent increase. Most young renters just pay up or move out, but they have the right to challenge it at the First-tier Tribunal (Property Chamber) if the new rent is above the market rate. The official forms are daunting, and building a solid evidence pack yourself takes hours. This service pulls comparable rents from Rightmove, Zoopla, and the Valuation Office Agency, then molds them into a professional witness statement and a completed tribunal application. It uses the same approach as property tax appeal services that help homeowners fight overassessment — transplanted here to help Gen Z renters keep their homes. For £99, you get a submission that sounds like it came from a surveyor, and you only pay if you go ahead.

A Gen Z worker renting an assured shorthold tenancy who has just received a rent increase notice that feels way above the local market. They pay a fixed fee to avoid a £1,200-a-year increase, and the cost is a fraction of the potential saving.
