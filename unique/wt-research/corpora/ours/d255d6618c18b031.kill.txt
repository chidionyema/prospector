RCFE BillSweep — audit your parent's assisted living bill and claw back every charge the state says is illegal.

A fixed-fee service that reviews your parent’s California assisted living bill, spots illegal charges, and sends citation-backed dispute letters that get your money back.

Adult children caring for parents in residential care facilities for the elderly (RCFEs) are often billed for things they never agreed to or that California law prohibits — like fees for staff training, charges for activities the resident can't attend, or medication management markups that conflict with the admission contract. A solo operator equipped with a growing database of common billing violations and AI that parses facility invoices against Title 22 regulations can quickly flag every improper line item. The operator then generates a formal dispute letter citing the exact regulatory section, demanding a refund. Facilities usually settle quickly because a complaint to the California Department of Social Services can trigger an inspection, and they would rather refund a few hundred dollars than risk a citation. The service costs a flat $149 per audit — far less than the $500+ in typical overcharges it recovers. The operator’s win rates compound because every resolved case adds new violation patterns to the proprietary dataset, making future audits faster and more precise.

The adult child who is the primary care decision-maker and handles the parent's finances. They pay the audit fee directly, typically after seeing a second bill that just doesn’t add up.
