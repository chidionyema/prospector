FuneralFund Desk – gets the DWP funeral payment denied to primary carers, for a fixed fee.

A fixed-fee service that overturns a rejected Social Fund Funeral Payment, rebuilding the carer’s application with a DWP‑proof evidence pack and a data‑backed appeal argument.

A primary carer who has just lost the person they looked after – often an elderly parent or disabled relative – must arrange a funeral and apply to the Department for Work and Pensions for a Funeral Expenses Payment. The DWP rejects a large proportion of these claims on details of the estate, the claimant’s relationship, or the funeral costs. The carer, exhausted and grieving, usually gives up. This service acts as a transaction broker: the carer signs over authority and we compile a mandatory‑reconsideration pack that mirrors exactly what the DWP’s own adjudicators accept, based on a proprietary dataset of successful appeals. The lens is adversarial invert: the DWP is institutionally incentivised to refuse, and the more aggressively it does so, the more valuable our dataset of what convinces them becomes. The solo operator runs it by automating the questionnaire intake and pack generation, with no need for a claims‑management licence because welfare benefit claims are outside the FCA’s regulated claims‑management scope. The fee is contingent – the carer pays us from the awarded lump sum – and small enough to be a no‑brainer compared with losing the payment entirely.
