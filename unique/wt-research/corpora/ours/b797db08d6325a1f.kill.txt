RentProof — the rental pack that gets your UK gig work accepted as income.

For gig workers rejected for UK rentals, we turn your Deliveroo and Uber Eats history into a landlord-accepted income pack in three days.

UK Gen Z gig workers — riders on Deliveroo, Uber Eats, Amazon Flex and Stuart, drivers on Uber and Bolt — are systematically rejected for tenancies because landlords want three months of payslips, an employer reference and a credit file showing regular salary. Their income is real (£1,500 to £3,000 a month is typical for a full-time rider in London) but invisible to the referencing tools landlords now use, including Experian, Canopy, Goodlord and RentProfile.

We are a productised service that pulls twenty-four months of platform data, reconciles it with HMRC records through the Government Gateway, and produces one PDF designed to pass these screening tools. The pack shows monthly net income, year-on-year trend, tax compliance status, a calculated affordability ratio against the rent being asked, and a counter-argument guide for the common objections landlords raise when they reject gig earners.

We charge £149 for the pack and £49 a year for refreshes. Distribution runs through partnerships with the places renters already search — OpenRent, SpareRoom, Zoopla and Rightmove's enquiry flow — plus paid acquisition on TikTok, Instagram and Reddit targeting riders and drivers by city.

The durable advantage is a proprietary dataset that grows with every pack sold: which platform combinations correlate with accepted applications, which landlord groups in which cities reject gig workers most often, which supporting documents (a Deliveroo Partner Confirmation, an Uber Driver Summary, a signed declaration of self-employment) swing borderline cases, and what rejection language AI referencing tools use. This dataset compounds, is not held by the credit-reference agencies or the landlord platforms, and lets us advise workers on the right evidence before they apply.
