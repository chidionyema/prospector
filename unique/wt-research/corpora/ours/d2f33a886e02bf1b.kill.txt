Retiree 'Get-Out-Clause' Property Viewing Service.

A solo agent-operator who physically attends property viewings for elderly home-sellers who live far away or are housebound, providing a detailed, impartial video report and negotiation support.

Retirees who downsize or move to be near family often sell their home while living elsewhere or are too frail to attend viewings. They desperately need a trustworthy, human 'proxy' on the ground to vet buyers, spot issues, and represent their interests—something no estate agent (who works for the seller's commission but not the seller's time) or video tour provides. This is a local, concierge service that commoditizes trust, not real estate expertise.

Retired UK homeowners (aged 65-78) selling their primary residence while living in a different city or abroad, or who are housebound due to mobility issues. They have significant home equity and a motivated need to sell at a fair price.

Post-pandemic, many retirees moved to coastal areas or abroad, but still hold UK property. The 2024 tax changes and stamp duty rules are driving downsizing. Estate agents have normalized 'virtual viewings' which retirees distrust for high-stakes sales. This fills the trust gap with a hyper-local, human presence.
