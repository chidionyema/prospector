GigLoan Autopilot – the income‑driven repayment optimizer for gig workers.

A SaaS that automatically picks the lowest student loan payments for freelancers and gig workers, recertifies their income each year, and tracks forgiveness progress.

Gig workers with federal student loans lose thousands by staying in the wrong repayment plan. Income‑driven repayment (IDR) plans like PAYE, REPAYE, and IBR can slash monthly bills to $0, but defaults and manual red tape push people away. This SaaS connects directly to your gig platforms and bank accounts, pulls real‑time income data, and then models every IDR plan under the latest rules to show the cheapest path—instantly. It also monitors your income for recertification deadlines, files the paperwork automatically, and tracks your qualifying payments toward forgiveness, so you never miss a deadline. This borrows the way robo‑advisors continuously optimize investment portfolios against market conditions, and applies it to student debt—treating the ever‑shifting mix of gig income and federal rules as a portfolio of repayment liabilities to be rebalanced automatically. The tool digs a moat through switching cost: once a user links six gig apps and three loan servicers, they can’t easily replicate that setup elsewhere. And as the tool collects thousands of anonymized repayment outcomes across gig sectors, it builds a proprietary dataset that lets it predict which plan will actually cost the least for someone with your income volatility—something no free government tool can do.

Gig workers, self‑employed creatives, and delivery riders—people aged 18–27 who carry federal student debt and have income that jumps from month to month. They pay a flat monthly subscription ($9–$15) because the software saves them far more than that every month by optimizing their IDR plan.

The federal student loan payment pause ended in late 2023, throwing 28 million borrowers back into repayment just as gig work participation hit an all‑time high. The Biden administration’s new SAVE plan and one‑time payment‑count adjustments make IDR more valuable than ever, but also more complicated. Meanwhile, gig platforms still issue 1099‑K forms with a year‑long lag, making it almost impossible for a rideshare or delivery worker to prove their current income to a loan servicer without a tool like this.
