PlankLock – The Builder's Material Price Cap.

A solo-operated service that lets small builders lock in today's price for key building materials on upcoming projects; if the market price soars, the difference is paid out, for a per-project fee.

Small builders often lose profit when material prices spike after quoting a fixed price to clients. Borrowing from commodity futures hedging (cross-sector: finance), this transfers the risk to the operator, who uses a proprietary AI model of local price trends to price the risk and manage a pooled reserve.

Self-employed builders and small contracting firms bidding on residential renovations.

Post-pandemic supply chain disruptions have made material prices unpredictable; inflation squeezes margins; builders seek ways to quote fixed prices without exposure.
