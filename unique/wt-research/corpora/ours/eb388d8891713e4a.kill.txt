FenceLine – The LiDAR Quote Engine for Solo Fence Installers.

A LiDAR-based app that instantly generates a material list, cost estimate, and cut list for residential wood fence jobs, accounting for local ordinance restrictions and live lumber prices.

Each new municipality added deepens the data moat—competitors would need months of manual research to replicate.
