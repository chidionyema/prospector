Pooled catalog licensing to AI labs for online stores.

A pooled licensing desk that signs your Shopify catalog into structured AI-training-data deals and pays you the net fee every release cycle.

Online stores hold something AI labs now have to licence rather than scrape: structured commercial product data with verified provenance. EU AI Act Article 53 (in force August 2025) and California AB 2013 (effective 2026) require AI model providers serving US or EU users to publish a training-data summary that names each source. Without consent-backed data, every model release opens the lab to EU AI Office action, California AG enforcement, or FTC inquiry under Section 5. Individual storefront operators cannot structure these deals: too small for AI-lab procurement to engage, no legal team, no way to publish an auditable training-data summary. A pooled licensing desk recruits hundreds of merchants into one panel, extracts each catalog in a consented licence-clean format, and contracts once with each AI lab on behalf of the pool. The desk earns per signed licence (typically 25 percent of the gross), with the remainder split pro rata to catalog contribution. The deal recurs on the major-model-release cycle (every six to twelve months per lab) and on shopping-agent skill updates (every one to three months). The pool compounds: more merchants on the panel means a more attractive catalog range for the next lab, which pulls more merchants, which makes the panel stickier. The desk pays out net quarterly and issues each merchant a contract reference and annual statement suitable for the merchant's own data-disclosure notice. The work the model performs inside the desk is the catalog extraction, provenance logging, licence preparation and per-deal reconciliation that would otherwise require a copyright licensing paralegal and a junior data-licensing counsel. The desk runs as a one-operator business with one legal contractor on retainer and a small contracting relationship with the model provider. This borrows the way collective rights organisations licence songwriters to streaming platforms, and applies it to commercial product catalogs in an era where statute now requires that licensing to be auditable.

US-based online store operators on Shopify, Amazon, BigCommerce and Walmart Marketplace who maintain a current catalog of one hundred SKUs or more and want a recurring revenue line from data the catalog already produces.

Three things converged in 2024 to 2026. EU AI Act Article 53 went into force in August 2025 and obliges AI providers to publish training-data summaries. California AB 2013 takes effect in 2026 and adds a state-level transparency mandate on top. AI shopping agents (ChatGPT Shopping, Google AI Mode, Perplexity Shopping) moved from preview to production, shifting demand from training crawls to structured continuously-licensed feeds. Reported institutional licensing deals (Reddit-Google at over sixty million dollars a year, Stack Overflow-OpenAI, Shutterstock's contributor program) establish a clearing-price range individual merchants cannot capture on their own.
