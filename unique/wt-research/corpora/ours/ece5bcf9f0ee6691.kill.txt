Making Tax Digital integration pack for UK accounting SaaS.

A reference integration and test harness that lets UK accounting software ship HMRC Making Tax Digital submissions on the quarterly deadline, not six months late.

From April 2026, HMRC's Making Tax Digital for Income Tax regime requires landlords and sole traders with income over £50,000 to file quarterly updates through compatible software, and from April 2027 the threshold falls to £30,000. UK accounting SaaS vendors must integrate with HMRC's MTD APIs to remain viable, but HMRC's developer documentation is fragmented and the test environment is brittle.

A vertical tool for engineering leads at UK accounting SaaS startups ships a maintained, versioned client library, a sandbox test harness with synthetic agent scenarios, and a regression suite mapped to HMRC's published failure modes. Each release is regression-tested against HMRC's spec and against failure patterns the tool has accumulated across its customer base.

The long-term edge is a maintained SDK, test harness and HMRC-error pattern library that engineering leads cannot reproduce by reading HMRC's docs. The first-party alternative (HMRC's MTD developer documentation at developer.service.hmrc.gov.uk) is the spec, not a maintained integration; bespoke consultancies charge forty to a hundred thousand pounds per integration. A subscription SDK at a few hundred pounds a month per vendor, plus a usage tier, is the edge consultancies cannot match at scale.
