FlockBook — the small egg producer's per-flock record-keeping and salmonella testing kit.

A physical binder and digital dashboard that keeps UK small-egg producers compliant with DEFRA rules, with automatic salmonella testing reminders and a flock performance tracker.

Small egg producers—those keeping fewer than 50 birds or selling at the farm gate—must comply with the Egg Marketing Standards Regulations and the National Control Programme for salmonella. They need to keep detailed records of flock treatments, egg sales, and test schedules, but existing options are either generic paper notebooks or expensive farm-management software built for large commercial flocks. FlockBook is a purpose-built physical binder containing pre-printed log sheets, pre-addressed sample submission forms to APHA-approved labs, and a unique QR code that unlocks a simple web dashboard. The dashboard automatically sends SMS reminders for mandatory salmonella testing based on the producer's flock size and the sampling frequency required by APHA. The kit is sold online, printed and shipped on demand with no inventory. The digital companion is built on a no-code platform, keeping the solo operator's overhead minimal. Over time, the aggregated and anonymised data from many small flocks—covering treatment efficacy, testing outcomes, and production patterns—becomes a proprietary dataset that can be sold as benchmarking reports to industry newsletters, feed suppliers, or even Defra. Producers face a real switching cost once a full year of flock records lives inside the platform.

UK small egg producers—hobbyist keepers, micro-farms, and smallholders who sell eggs at the farm gate or through local honesty boxes. They pay a one-off £29 for the binder and first year of digital access, then £12 annually to keep the dashboard active and receive reminders.
