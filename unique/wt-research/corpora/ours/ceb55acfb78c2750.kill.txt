AdaptedValue — Sell your adapted home and beat the council's care-funding valuation.

A specialist estate broker for carers selling homes with disability adaptations, armed with proprietary sale-price data to fight council overvaluations and attract pre-vetted buyers.

A primary carer who sells the family home (often to fund care or downsize) faces a financial assessment from the local council that routinely overvalues adapted properties. Standard valuations ignore the discount that stairlifts, wetrooms and widened doorways impose on marketability. AdaptedValue acts as a transaction broker: it markets the property exclusively to a pool of buyers who specifically need adapted homes, secures a sale, and simultaneously provides the carer with a data-backed valuation challenge pack to present to the council.

The primary carer pays a success fee on completion of the sale — typically 1.5% of the sale price, which is competitive and more than offset by the higher net proceeds from the valuation challenge.

Council adult social care budgets are under severe strain, leading to increasingly aggressive financial assessments that treat adapted homes as standard assets. At the same time, the number of adapted homes is rising as the population ages, yet no mainstream estate agent captures the evidence of how adaptations depress sale prices. This gap is widening.
