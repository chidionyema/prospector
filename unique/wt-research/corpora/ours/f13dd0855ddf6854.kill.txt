Pension Death Benefit Nomination Atlas – The Retiree's Dispute Database.

A searchable report series and dataset that analyses UK Pensions Ombudsman and court rulings where pension trustees ignored member nominations, giving retiring DC scheme members evidence to craft airtight expressions of wish and challenge unfair decisions, using a proprietary library of successful and failed wording patterns.

Pension death benefit disputes are rising as trust‑based schemes retain broad discretion. The Pensions Regulator and ombudsman publish only summaries, not a granular case corpus revealing which wording failed and which succeeded. A solo operator can compile this jurisprudence into a searchable, practice‑oriented database, enabling members to craft ‘litigation‑proof’ nominations. The regulator cannot and will not teach members how to structurally bypass trustee discretion.
