PitchSweep – The freelance writer's weekly brief alert that catches hidden public-sector copywriting work.

A weekly paid email that sifts UK public procurement portals for copywriting, content design and editorial freelance opportunities, so you stop missing the quiet briefs.

This is a paid newsletter for freelance writers and content designers who want a steady flow of public-sector leads. Every week it scans Contracts Finder, Find a Tender, and local council e-tendering systems, pulling out Briefs that are too small for agencies but perfectly sized for a solo practitioner. Each item includes a plain-English summary of the requirement, the named buyer, budget if stated, and a one-paragraph tip on how the brief maps to typical freelance deliverables.

Most public-sector copywriting work never reaches the networks freelancers rely on. It sits on platforms that are hard to navigate and written in procurement language. By curating only the briefs a writer can actually win, PitchSweep acts like the trade magazines that used to print tender notices, but filtered for the gig economy. The operator builds a crawler and a growing database of past awards, which feeds future curation and improves hit rates over time.

This borrows the model of specialist analyst briefings used in the defence sector, where private insight services extract signal from government procurement noise. The durable wedge is the compounding dataset of past award values, decision patterns and buyer behaviour — a proprietary data asset that gets stronger with every issue. A one-person team can run the crawler, write the summaries and manage a subscriber list through a platform like Substack or Ghost, with payment built in.

Freelance copywriters, content strategists and editorial consultants who want to diversify their client base beyond referrals and who see public-sector briefs as a reliable, under-accessed income stream. They pay £12 a month for early access to the weekly drop.
