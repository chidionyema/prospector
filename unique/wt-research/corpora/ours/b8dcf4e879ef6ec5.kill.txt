NCSC cloud packs for UK G-Cloud bidders.

A fixed-fee evidence pack that maps a UK dev agency's architecture to all 14 NCSC Cloud Security Principles, ready to drop into a G-Cloud or NHS Digital bid.

The NCSC publishes 14 Cloud Security Principles covering data-in-transit protection, asset protection and resilience, supply chain, operational management and the rest. G-Cloud buyers (Crown Commercial Service, NHS Digital, DWP, Ministry of Justice) require suppliers to evidence alignment with these principles, and the assessor typically rebuilds the evidence manually for each bid.

A productized service that, for a fixed fee, takes the agency's reference architecture (Azure landing zone, AWS landing zone, GCP organisation, hybrid) and returns within seven working days a 14-principle evidence pack with the named control references, the assessor-grade language, and a lot-by-lot mapping for G-Cloud, NHS Digital, DWP and MoJ, would replace a £6,000 to £15,000 cyber consultancy pre-bid engagement.

The compounding asset is a corpus of NCSC-aligned architecture patterns tied to specific UK G-Cloud lots and named buyer requirements. Each engagement adds to the corpus and lets the next bid's evidence pack be assembled faster.

The first-party alternative is the NCSC's own cloud security guidance, which publishes the 14 principles but not pre-filled evidence tied to a specific architecture. The Big 4 (PWC, EY, KPMG, Deloitte) charge £20,000 to £50,000 for a CSP-aligned engagement and only serve enterprise clients; agencies bidding on £500,000 to £5,000,000 G-Cloud lots need a fixed-fee, fast-turnaround pack sized to their bid, with lot-by-lot mapping for CCS, NHS Digital, DWP and MoJ.
