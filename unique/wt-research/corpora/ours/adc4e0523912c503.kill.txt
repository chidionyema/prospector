ProbeProof — the mobile calibration crew that keeps your small food business inspection-ready.

A regional van crew visits your kitchen each quarter, calibrates every probe and scale on the spot, and leaves UKAS-traceable certificates your biggest buyers accept.

Small UK food producers — cheesemakers, jam makers, micro-brewers, charcuterie makers — need their temperature probes, pH meters, refractometers and weighing scales calibrated against a traceable standard. Without proof, supermarket buyers and B2B wholesalers walk away. Today they post instruments to a lab and lose them for days, replace kit too often, or run uncalibrated.

We bring a van crew to your premises on a fixed rota. Two trained engineers spend two hours calibrating every instrument against UKAS-traceable references, leave a digital certificate per device, and store a per-client history in our database. By visit four you have a two-year calibration trail in one place — exactly what a SALSA or BRC audit asks for.

The wedge is the calibration history itself. Each visit compounds the value of staying with us, so switching costs grow quarterly. The trained engineer skill, the in-van bench standards and the documented procedure are technical IP a new entrant can't replicate by simply hiring.

Why this survives the lab alternative: sending instruments away means days of downtime per device, multiplied across every probe in your business. A cheesemaker with twelve probes cannot afford two weeks offline. The lab also charges £300+ per instrument; we charge a flat subscription covering everything.

UKAS itself does not compete: it accredits labs, it does not run a commercial service. SALSA and BRC set the rules but do not sell calibration. So we sit in the gap.
