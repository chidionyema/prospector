LostBid.ai, the federal AI procurement loss-autopsy index.

A private score for federal AI vendors showing which AI bids historically lost at GAO protest, and why, so the ops manager can skip dead-end RFPs before they bid.

An ops manager at a small or mid-sized GovTech vendor (50 to 500 employees, 5 to 50 million dollars annual revenue) selling AI capabilities to federal civilian agencies — HHS, Treasury, GSA, DoD — does bid or no-bid analysis on 5 to 15 federal AI solicitations each month. Today that ops manager has two bad options: scrape GAO bid protest decisions manually and cross-reference FPDS.gov contract awards, or pay 30,000 dollars or more per year for GovWin or Deltek, which tags by NAICS code but not by AI capability type. Neither tool ties AI bids to specific protest loss-causes — an agency rejecting a vendor for failing an M-25-21 attestation clause, for example.

By systematically scraping GAO bid protests since 2018, FPDS awards, and SAM.gov AI opportunity listings, then tagging each by AI capability type (natural language processing, computer vision, predictive analytics, robotic process automation) and aggregating loss-cause patterns, the tool builds a private dataset that compounds with every new AI procurement cycle the customer runs through it. It works like a reinsurance underwriter's loss-history index pointed at the regulator's side: as OMB M-25-21 (January 2025) tightens federal AI procurement and agencies gain more discretion to reject vendors, the loss data becomes more valuable. GAO, as an adjudicator, structurally cannot publish which AI vendors will lose next, and agency Chief AI Officers cannot share evaluation methodologies publicly because they are bound by procurement integrity rules in the Federal Acquisition Regulation.

Pricing sits at 300 to 800 dollars per month per ops manager subscription, plus 2,000 dollars per pre-bid deep-dive report — well below GovWin's enterprise tier and within reach for a 50-person GovTech vendor. The moat is proprietary data, because the cross-reference of AI capability tag to protest loss-cause to FPDS award outcome is a fixed engineering cost to build that GovWin has never paid and that a new entrant can build once and serve every subsequent customer with.
