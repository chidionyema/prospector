EHCPBridge — The Parent-Carer's SEND Tribunal Bundle & Hearing Broker.

A fixed-fee transaction broker who prepares the SEND tribunal evidence bundle, lodges the appeal, and represents the parent-carer at First-tier Tribunal hearing, anchored on a proprietary per-local-authority dataset of refusal patterns, panel preferences and winning argument templates.

Lens: broaden + invert. EHCP applications have risen roughly 60% post-Covid while local authority high-needs SEND budgets are down ~15% in real terms, driving refusal rates and tribunal registrations to record highs. Citizens Advice, SENDIASS and IPSEA provide free advice but cannot represent at tribunal; specialist SEN solicitors charge £3,000–6,000+ per case. A solo broker with a proprietary per-LA dataset of tribunal outcomes, panel composition, hearing-officer preferences and successful argument templates — built via scripted FOI requests to every English upper-tier/unitary authority and automated scraping of public First-tier Tribunal decisions — can deliver a single £1,200 per case appeal bundle plus hearing representation for the ~12,000–15,000 families per year who register a SEND appeal, with broader pre-tribunal interventions (assessment refusal, plan inadequacy, annual-review downgrade, phase-transfer refusal) layered in at £250–450 each. The 'invert' element: the local authority is the adversarial duty-imposer; it cannot structurally advise or represent the parent against itself, and HMCTS is a neutral venue. The 'broaden' element wraps the entire SEND statutory value chain — refusal to assess, refusal to plan, plan inadequacy, annual-review downgrade, phase transfer — into one broker relationship per family. Distribution is owned via SEND-parent Facebook groups, the National Autistic Society, Contact, Mencap, IPSEA's parent network, and SEO for 'EHCP appeal [city]' terms. The dataset compounds with every case, building a per-LA win-rate index no incumbent can match.

UK parent-carer of a disabled child (typically aged 0–25) facing a local authority's refusal to assess, refusal to issue a plan, plan inadequacy, annual-review downgrade or phase-transfer denial. Solvent because tribunal orders can include 30+ hours of additional SEND provision plus costs orders of £200–£6,000; desperate because the child is losing educational hours weekly; time-poor because Carer's Leave Act (April 2024) and tribunal-hearing preparation overhead compete with full-time caring. Annual pool: ~12,000–15,000 tribunal registrations plus ~30,000–40,000 pre-tribunal disputes that settle when the parent turns up with a strong bundle.

(1) Post-Covid EHCP application surge of ~60% with LA refusal rates at record highs. (2) Tribunal backlog exceeding 6 months at many hearing centres. (3) 2024–25 SEND Code of Practice statutory guidance revision. (4) Carer's Leave Act (April 2024) crystallised parents' time poverty into a measurable new right. (5) Ofsted/CQC SEND area inspections now grading LAs partly on tribunal outcomes, sharpening adversarial LA behaviour. (6) ~70% of cases that reach hearing succeed for parents with representation, but SENDIASS is under-resourced (most services cover only one LA each and cannot represent) and paid advocates are fragmented and not data-driven.
