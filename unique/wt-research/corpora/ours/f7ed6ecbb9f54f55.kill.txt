BedFall Bench — The Independent Care-Home Owner's Per-Bedroom Furniture Entrapment & Gap-Measurement Bench.

A camera-and-caliper vertical tool that lets a single-site care-home owner measure, log and re-prove every bed rail, hoist and window-restrictor dimensional gap in their building against the MHRA/CQC entrapment zones — producing a per-bedroom dimensional ledger that becomes the home's own defence asset when an incident or inspection lands.

First-principles: strip away how this is 'solved' today (a laminated policy folder and a maintenance man's memory) and the raw pain is dimensional — a bed-rail entrapment or window-restrictor failure is a millimetre problem, and the owner has no per-bedroom record of millimetres over time. Nobody sells a measurement instrument for this; they sell paperwork. The tool ships a printed reference target + phone photogrammetry that converts a bedside photo into the seven MHRA entrapment zone clearances, timestamped per bed asset. Value compounds because the ledger is longitudinal: a mattress replaced, a rail bent, a restrictor loosened all show as dimensional drift the owner can act on before a coroner does. The proprietary asset is the cross-home dimensional dataset of which bed/mattress/rail COMBINATIONS drift out of tolerance and how fast — data no manufacturer holds because no manufacturer sees rivals' mattresses on its own beds.

Owner-operators of single-site or two-site UK independent residential care homes with 15-45 beds (roughly 6,000 such homes), who personally sign the CQC provider information return and personally carry the liability — £39/month per home plus £8 per bed on initial baseline capture.

MHRA bed-rail safety alerts and post-Covid CQC inspection backlog clearing have pushed dimensional entrapment evidence into inspection conversations, while the collapse of block local-authority contracts has left small independents without the group-level estates function that chains use to absorb this — they must self-evidence with no facilities team.

The passages confirm bed-rail entrapment is a live, regulated dimensional problem — MHRA audits govern entrapment zone checks (Zones 1–4) and guidance treats rail/mattress gaps, including between split rails, as a real safety hazard [2b721b6b41b29263][30111f3c732edb50][85a09dd085e217f3].
