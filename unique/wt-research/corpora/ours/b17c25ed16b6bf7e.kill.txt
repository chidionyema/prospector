SeatSqueaky Clean – the mobile car seat deep cleaning chain for busy parents.

A local chain of mobile car seat cleaning vans that deep-clean and sanitize the mess kids make, on a schedule parents set.

Parents dread the moment a juice box explodes into a car seat, because deep‑cleaning a car seat properly takes hours and involves wrestling with straps, buckles, and mysterious old crumbs. Existing services like auto detailers rarely touch the under‑side or sanitize to daycare standards. This chain solves the problem by sending a trained technician in a branded van to the parent’s home, workplace, or daycare parking lot. The tech removes the seat, vacuums, steam‑cleans, sanitizes with a hospital‑grade quaternary disinfectant, and reinstalls the seat to the manufacturer’s specs — all in about 30 minutes.

It works because every parent with a child under 5 faces this chore, and those with two incomes often value the time saved at $40–$60 an hour far more than the service cost. A single van can easily complete 5–7 jobs a day at $89 per clean, yielding over $2,000 in weekly revenue with relatively low materials cost. The chain expands by adding vans and technicians, each covering their own territory; central dispatch manages appointments through a simple website and text reminders.

The unit economics work without a massive ad spend because word‑of‑mouth among parent groups is strong and the service is highly visible when the branded van sits outside a home. The small team can start with one van and owner‑operated technician, then hire and train additional technicians as demand grows. The business can operate in any suburban region with a concentration of families with young children; in California, targeting cities like Irvine, Pasadena, and Walnut Creek gives a dense, solvent market. The durability comes from a compounding brand of reliable, safe, and convenient service that a generalist mobile detailer can’t match because they lack the car seat installation training and the focused speed.

Parents of children in car seats (birth to about 5 years), particularly dual‑income households with disposable income and no time to scrub. They pay per cleaning, or subscribe to a monthly or bi‑monthly plan for $25/month including a basic clean.
