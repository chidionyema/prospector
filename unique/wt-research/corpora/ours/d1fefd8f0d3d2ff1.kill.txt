Gig Platform Trust Score.

A weekly data brief that tracks and rates the actual reliability, pay delays, and unfair deactivation patterns of gig platforms—using only worker-generated signals scraped from public forums and social media—so a rider knows which app to prioritize today.

Gig platforms wield immense data power over workers by rating and deactivating them, yet workers have no equivalent collective intelligence about which platforms are decent. By inverting the surveillance, a solo operator can scrape Reddit, GigCX communities, Twitter/X, and app-store review sentiment (filtered for worker-relevant keywords) to build a proprietary Platform Dependability Index. This insight is valuable to riders and drivers who multi-app and can instantly shift hours to the most dependable platform in a given city.

London-based Uber Eats and Deliveroo riders (18–27) who multi-app and have a few pounds a month to spend on a tool that increases their daily take-home.

Gig platforms are increasingly aggressive with opaque pay cuts, unfair deactivations, and hidden surge policies; a million UK workers now rely on them. Regulators can't move fast, and platforms structurally won't share real reliability data. Scraped, curated intelligence from the worker's perspective is a new asymmetric asset.
