MarginCheck — the pharmacy dispenser's real-time loss alert for NHS prescriptions.

Flags every NHS prescription that would lose money before you dispense it, using the latest Drug Tariff and your own purchase costs.

Independent pharmacy owners lose hours each month cross-referencing the NHS Drug Tariff PDF against their wholesaler invoices to find which items will be reimbursed at less than cost. When they miss one, every jar dispensed widens the hole. MarginCheck is a web-based tool where you enter the drug name, pack size, and your purchase price once. It then pulls the latest reimbursement from the tariff (updated monthly) and shows you a live margin per item. For recurring prescriptions, it sends an alert when a tariff price drops below your cost floor. By turning a fuzzy monthly panic into a five-second check, the tool stops profit leaks before the label prints. It works because no one else — not the NHS, not the wholesalers — has an incentive to surface the loss per dispense. The sole operator builds a paid-subscription utility that compounds a dataset of tariff volatility and purchase-cost benchmarks, making each customer’s margin history more valuable over time.

Independent pharmacy owners (typically single-shop operators with 1–4 staff) who do their own buying and cannot afford a full-time procurement analyst.

The NHS community pharmacy contract is under severe funding pressure, with flat global sums and rising costs. The Drug Tariff changes monthly and over half of all items see a price adjustment each month. Many pharmacies are unknowingly dispensing at a loss, and closures are accelerating. A simple tool that turns tariff data into a live margin check is urgently needed.
