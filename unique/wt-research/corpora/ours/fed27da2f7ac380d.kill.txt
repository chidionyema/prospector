The Adoption Match Atlas.

A personalised matching intelligence brief for approved UK adopters, built on a FOI-derived, quarterly-compounding dataset of per-agency wait times, placement-preference profiles and family-finding social-worker turnover across England's Regional Adoption Agencies and Voluntary Adoption Agencies.

Approved adopters sit in a 6–18 month 'matching' phase after panel approval with near-zero visibility into which of the 31 Regional Adoption Agencies (plus ~30 Voluntary Adoption Agencies) is realistically going to place them, what specific demographic criteria that agency's family-finding team is prioritising this quarter, or whether their allocated social worker is about to leave. By running rolling FOI requests against every RAA and VAA (via WhatDoTheyKnow), aggregating published panel minutes, disruption-rate disclosures and adopter-feedback surveys, and combining this with the adopter's own profile (age range, openness to sibling groups, contact-birth-family willingness, faith-school preference, ethnicity-of-child preferences), the Atlas produces a fixed-fee brief that (1) ranks each agency in their catchment by realistic match probability for their stated profile, (2) flags agencies in 'matching slowdown' due to social-worker turnover, and (3) supplies a 'profile polish pack' addressing the specific vocabulary, training certificates and references each slow agency is documented to weight. The dataset compounds every quarter as new FOI returns arrive, new placements are published, and social-worker churn data refreshes — late entrants cannot backfill historical panel minutes.

Approved UK adopters in the matching phase — typically dual-income professional couples aged 32–46 with £50–90k household income, who have already invested £10–30k in legal/social-work fees and will pay £175–£295 for a brief that materially shortens their wait or improves their match outcome.

Three concurrent signals: (1) the 2024–26 Regional Adoption Agencies consolidation is creating wider intra-region wait-time variance than ever before — adopters in matching phases are being silently reallocated to new agencies mid-process; (2) post-placement disruption rates rose to 7.4% in 2024 (DfE) and agencies are tightening adopter-profile screening, increasing the value of profile polish; (3) the Information Commissioner's Office has tightened FOI response deadlines to 18 working days, and several RAAs (Adopt London North, Adoption West, One Adoption North West) have published 2024 placement-rate disclosures under FOI that expose dramatic inter-agency variance no central authority publishes.
