PA money transmitter filings, for fintech AI.

Generates PA Money Transmitter Business Act license applications for PA-based fintech AI vendors launching payment, lending or crypto products.

This is an AI-native tool for Compliance Leads at PA-based fintech AI startups (AI lending, AI wealth, AI crypto custody, payment AI) applying for a Pennsylvania money transmitter license. The PA Money Transmitter Business Act (7 Pa.C.S. § 6101 et seq., enacted as Act 36 of 2018) requires a 100-page application including a surety bond, an AML program, a business plan, and three years of projected financials. Today this is done by a fintech licensing lawyer at $300 to $500 an hour over 60 to 90 hours.

The tool replaces 50 of those hours at a $1,500 fixed fee, producing a packet ready to file with PA DOBS. It draws on a proprietary database of past PA DOBS deficiency letter patterns that pre-empts the questions the Department asks in review. This borrows the way FCC spectrum licensing packets are built from a checklist of technical exhibits and reviewed by an internal scoring panel, and applies it to PA MTL applications reviewed by PA DOBS under the same discretionary scoring model.

It works because the PA DOBS publishes its MTL checklist but does not publish the reasons prior applications were sent back, and the three-year financial projection is the single most common source of delay. The free PA DOBS application form is on the DOBS website; a fintech licensing lawyer does the rest. The wedge is the proprietary database of past PA DOBS deficiency letter patterns, which PA DOBS cannot publish because of applicant confidentiality. The strongest free alternative is the published PA DOBS MSB-1 checklist; it cannot capture this value because the checklist does not tell you what to write in each section, and the Department reviews each section independently.
