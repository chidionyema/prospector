Door supervisors, security guards, and CCTV operators must renew their SIA licence every three years, providing proof of refresher training. The online process is fiddly, and minor errors cause weeks of delay, leaving them unable to work. This borrows the model of a visa agency: a specialist desk that knows the exact rules, checks all documents, and submits directly through the government portal. The solo operator uses a checklist-driven workflow, keeps copies of common training provider certificates, and tracks each application until the licence is granted.
