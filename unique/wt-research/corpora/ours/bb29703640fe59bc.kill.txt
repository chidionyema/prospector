The Hospital Charity Care Application Advocate.

A done-for-you service that applies for hospital charity care, using a proprietary database of each hospital’s income thresholds, documentation tricks, and appeal processes, and takes a contingency fee only on debt avoided.

Analogous to grant writing for nonprofits—transplanting expert application writing and institutional knowledge into consumer healthcare. Non-profit hospitals are required to offer charity care, but the programs are deliberately invisible and labyrinthine. A solo advocate armed with a growing database of hospital-specific thresholds and document playbooks navigates this path adversarially, turning a dead debt into cash for gig workers.

Uninsured Gen Z gig workers in states with aggressive charity care laws (e.g., CA, NY) who receive a hospital bill they cannot afford.

Medical debt is the leading cause of bankruptcy for young Americans. New federal rules (effective 2024) mandate hospitals post financial assistance policies online, making systematic scraping feasible. Post-pandemic cost-of-living pressures make recovery of every dollar urgent, while hospitals face increased scrutiny over billing practices.
