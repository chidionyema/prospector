SOPPA evidence pack for Illinois AI tutoring vendors.

A fixed-fee pack that gives an AI tutoring vendor the SOPPA addendum, data flow, and parent notice an Illinois public school district demands.

Plain version: Illinois public school districts must vet every edtech vendor that touches student data under SOPPA (Student Online Personal Protection Act, 105 ILCS 85), and the Illinois State Board of Education (ISBE) has published updated SOPPA guidance that districts now bake into their RFPs. Most AI tutoring vendors cannot answer the SOPPA questionnaire in the procurement window the district allows, and lose the deal because their sales lead is rewriting the same boilerplate addendum for the hundredth time.

This borrows the way food allergen disclosure packs work for K-12 nutrition programs (a fixed-format artifact a food vendor hands a school district to satisfy a state-level mandate), and applies it to SOPPA evidence packs for AI tutoring vendors selling to Illinois public school districts.

Why it works: each new Illinois district RFP triggers the same SOPPA artifact prep.
