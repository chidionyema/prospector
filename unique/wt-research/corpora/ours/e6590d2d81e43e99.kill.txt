The EHCP Request Architect.

A web tool that guides parents of children with special educational needs through building a legally structured, evidence-backed request for an Education Health and Care (EHC) needs assessment, powered by a proprietary dataset of successful tribunal cases and local authority decision patterns.

Parents battle Local Authorities for EHCPs without knowing what arguments score points. A solo operator can FOI-request EHCP tribunal outcomes and initial refusal reasons, then encode them into an AI engine that generates a personalised, evidence-anchored request pack—changing the parent into an informed, adversarial negotiator from the first letter.
