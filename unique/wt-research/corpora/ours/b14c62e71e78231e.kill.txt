SAM 4845 AI risk assessment for AI startups.

An AI tool that completes the California State Administrative Manual (SAM) 4845.5 AI Risk Assessment for AI startups bidding on California state contracts.

California SAM 4845.5 (effective June 2024) requires every California state agency to complete an AI Risk Assessment before procuring AI, and every AI vendor seeking state contracts to disclose the same documentation. The deliverable is a 4-section assessment covering data sources, model risk, bias testing, and human oversight. The model does the work of AI consultants at Booz Allen, Deloitte, and Slalom who charge $400-700/hr per assessment, and the work of solo consultants hired via CDTech's procurement roster. AI startups need to produce one before every California state RFP. The tool ingests the startup's product and policy documents, generates the SAM 4845.5 assessment, and maintains it through the contract lifecycle. The deliverable is a state-submittable risk assessment plus a version-locked evidence pack.
