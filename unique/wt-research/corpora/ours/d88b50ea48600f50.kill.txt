GrantGap Mapper — the retiree's personalised route through local and charity energy grants.

A data brief that finds every council fund, charity grant and supplier scheme you qualify for, and tells you exactly how to apply.

When the Government stopped the universal Winter Fuel Payment in July 2024, it left millions of pensioners facing higher bills without automatic help. The financial support that still exists is scattered across hundreds of local council schemes, energy supplier hardship funds and small charities — each with its own rules, windows and application forms. No single national service maps them all.

GrantGap Mapper is a data‑intelligence product that takes a retiree’s postcode, household income, health conditions and property details, cross‑checks them against a continuously updated database of every tangible energy‑cost relief pot in that area, and delivers a personalised action plan ranked by likelihood of success. The database is built by scraping council websites, charity guidelines and supplier pages, then structuring the rules into a matching engine.

This is an inversion of the usual welfare advice model. The state’s centralised safety net is shrinking precisely as energy poverty reporting rises, but central government will not build a tool that points citizens toward locally controlled, discretionary funds — doing so would highlight its own withdrawal. The value of GrantGap Mapper grows as more universal payments are chopped, because the remaining help becomes more fragmented and harder to find.

A solo operator can build and run the whole thing: a web scraper, a rule‑based engine and a PDF‑generated report sold for a fixed fee. The durable protection is proprietary data — a mapping of hyperlocal grant criteria that no single council, charity comparison site or benefits calculator compiles, because none has a commercial reason to do it nationally.
