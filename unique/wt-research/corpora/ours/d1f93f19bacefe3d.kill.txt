FitProof – The Personal Trainer's Results Translator.

A tool that converts fitness client wearable data and progress metrics into tangible ROI reports (health cost savings, longevity gains) to justify premium rates and upsell packages.

From first principles, personal trainers struggle to prove value beyond aesthetics, leading to price sensitivity and churn. By building a tool that ingests client data (sleep, steps, heart rate, workouts) and outputs a personalized 'health investment return' report based on actuarial health cost models, a solo operator gives trainers a powerful retention and upsell instrument. (Lens: first_principles).

Independent personal trainers with 20+ recurring clients, paying $29/month or $199/year.

Wearable adoption is high; health cost data is publicly available; AI can synthesize personalized reports; trainers seek to professionalize as coaching market tightens.
