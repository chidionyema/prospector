Denial Decoder — your Florida Medicare Advantage appeal win‑rate brief.

A single‑page report that gives the real chance of overturning a coverage denial for your exact condition and plan, using CMS’s own hidden numbers.

Over half of Florida’s seniors are in a Medicare Advantage plan, and every year thousands of claims for skilled nursing, rehab, or specialist treatment are denied as 'not medically necessary.' When a retiree appeals, the data shows that denials are overturned at wildly different rates depending on the diagnosis, the plan, and the level of review — but nobody hands you those odds. We pull the CMS Part C and Part D appeals outcomes by plan and diagnosis code (public but buried) and cross‑reference it with each plan’s complaint history. The output is a short, personalised brief: ‘For a COPD rehab denial with your plan, 38 of 100 appeals succeed at the first level; here’s the single strongest argument used by the winners.’

The adversarial inversion: the federal government sets the rules requiring plans to publish denial data, but it will never build a tool that helps an enrollee weaponise those numbers. Every time CMS tightens the marketing or network‑adequacy standards, more plans tighten their denials — and our archive becomes more valuable.
