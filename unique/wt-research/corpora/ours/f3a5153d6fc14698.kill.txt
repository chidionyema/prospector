PipeCheck – The Lead-in-Pipes Home Water Audit Kit.

A mail-in water test kit that builds a crowdsourced, street-level map of lead pipe risk, starting with anxious public sector homeowners in the UK.

By aggregating citizen-sourced test results into a hyperlocal, anonymized dataset that reveals plumbing contamination patterns invisible to utilities, PipeCheck transforms a simple test kit into a defensible public health data asset — creating a moat no single-test lab can replicate.
