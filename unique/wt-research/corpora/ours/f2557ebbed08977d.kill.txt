The Pipeline Index – the weekly forward-demand forecast for UK freelance creatives, built from public data signals so you can pitch before the herd.

A weekly report that forecasts freelance demand in design, writing and video by analysing public data, helping you find work before the rush.

This borrows the way commodity traders use forward-demand indices to lock in better prices, and applies it to the unpredictable feast-and-famine cycle of freelance creative work. Each week the report distils signals from Companies House filings of new marketing agencies, Arts Council grant awards that imply upcoming campaigns, ONS service-sector output trends, Contracts Finder opportunities, and planning applications for commercial shoots. The analysis translates these into a 4-week forward demand score per discipline, with an archive that grows into a proprietary dataset of lead-lag relationships — something a solo newcomer cannot quickly replicate. The format is a concise email and a simple dashboard built in a no‑code tool, making it fully automatable after initial signal discovery. Because the data is scraped from open UK sources and then uniquely correlated, the moat is not the raw data but the compounding accuracy of the forecast model over time.
