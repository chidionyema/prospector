ShiftScout – The Gig Worker's Platform Algorithm Decoder.

A data intelligence feed that reverse-engineers gig platform shift-allocation algorithms by aggregating micro-decision data from thousands of workers, delivering personalized schedules that maximize hourly earnings.

Gig platforms (Uber Eats, Deliveroo) operate black-box algorithms that assign orders and surging bonuses to maximize platform profit at the expense of worker predictability. By crowdsourcing granular shift-log data (time, location, acceptance, earnings) and applying causal inference, a solo operator can model the algorithm's behavioral triggers and sell a daily 'earnings-maximizing route' back to workers — effectively arbing the platform's opacity.
