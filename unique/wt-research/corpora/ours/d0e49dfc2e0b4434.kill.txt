CodeQuote – The Auto Repair Shop's Instant DTC‑to‑Quote Engine.

A vertical tool that reads a car’s diagnostic trouble codes and instantly produces a fixed‑price customer quote by combining live parts pricing, labour guides, and aggregated shop repair data, all while the service advisor is still plugging in the scanner.

First-principles pain: when a check‑engine light comes into an independent shop, the service advisor spends 10–20 minutes manually looking up codes, phoning parts stores, and consulting labor guides to build a quote — a delay that loses price‑sensitive customers to dealerships or quick‑lube chains. An AI that automates that 20 minutes into a 30‑second quote, using real‑time parts APIs and proprietary actual‑repair‑time data from participating shops, can lift conversion rates and reduce key‑to‑quote time dramatically.

Owner of an independent auto repair shop (3–12 bays, typically 2–8 mechanics) who directly feels the competitive pressure from chains and the daily friction of customers waiting for a price during diagnostics.

Modern OBD‑II tools can stream codes to a smartphone; vehicle parts pricing APIs are mature; and GPT‑class models can reliably map a DTC to a repair plan with a confidence score. Simultaneously, rising dealership prices have pushed more out‑of‑warranty vehicles into independent shops, creating a volume surge that makes quoting speed a real differentiator.

The passages show labour-time and DTC-code data are already commoditised: paid industry-standard tools (AutoData, HaynesPro) are entrenched in independent garages, while a free community-sourced database offers 700K+ labor times, DTC codes, and specs with API access from $49/mo, and another offers a free offline DTC lookup database. This undercuts the claim that repair-time-per-DTC data is a durable proprietary asset worth building a paid quoting tool around.
