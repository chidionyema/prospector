Controller-processor briefs for UK marketing agencies.

Signed Article 28 packs and audit trails for UK marketing agencies wiring in a third-party MarTech platform, ready for an ICO inspection.

Under UK GDPR Article 28, when a UK marketing agency plugs a third-party MarTech platform into its client work (Klaviyo, Segment, Hubspot, Mixpanel, Customer.io, Mailchimp and similar), the platform processes personal data on the agency's instruction. The agency needs a signed controller-processor contract meeting eight minimum clauses, with the platform's data-protection appendix attached — and each new integration needs its own signed pack with an audit trail.

A productised service generates the brief per integration: pulls the platform's published DPA terms, layers in the agency-specific instruction set, identifies the eight clauses and where the platform's published terms fall short, and produces the signed controller-processor pack with audit trail. Public source proving the pain is the ICO's 2023-24 Action Plan on Adtech and Consent Receipts, plus named enforcement notices on platform controllers who could not produce signed Article 28 packs (published on the ICO enforcement register, ico.org.uk).

The wedge is a running database of how each major MarTech platform phrases the eight Article 28 clauses, plus a per-agency integration log of signed controllers and known gaps. A new entrant today starts without the database; an incumbent with 18 months of client-platform pairings has 80 to 200 vetted pairings it can re-use across new briefs.
