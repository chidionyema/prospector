The Garden Office Power Broker.

A solo operator who sources, negotiates, and project-manages the installation of pre-fabricated garden offices for cash-strapped professional couples, taking a fixed fee from the installer rather than the buyer.

First-principles reframe: The friction for the squeezed middle persona isn't finding a garden office supplier—it's the paralyzing complexity of comparing quotes, verifying installers, and managing a small construction project while both partners work full-time. By acting as a niche distribution channel that pre-vets a curated set of 3-5 local installers, negotiates volume discounts and priority slots, and handles the buyer's coordination overhead for free, I extract value from the installer side (who gets a steady, high-intent lead flow they can't easily access) while the buyer gets a turnkey solution they'd otherwise never act on. The solo operator owns the relationship and the vetting process, creating switching cost for installers who depend on this lead source.

Garden office installers (prefab and bespoke) within a 30-mile radius of a single affluent commuter town—each pays a 10-15% commission on the project value (typically £15k-£40k) upon completion.

Post-pandemic, garden offices are the #1 home improvement for professionals who need dedicated workspace but can't move. However, the market is fragmented with hundreds of small installers, no dominant review platform, and a buying process that feels like hiring a contractor. The adjacent US market has seen 'garden office concierges' emerge; the UK lacks a trusted intermediary.
