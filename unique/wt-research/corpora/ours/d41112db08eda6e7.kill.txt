GigMortgage Passport – The gig worker's lender-ready income report, packaged for a fixed fee.

A fixed-fee service that transforms your chaotic gig-app earnings into a single, standardised income document mortgage lenders will accept.

This service builds a bespoke income dossier for a Gen Z gig worker who wants a mortgage. It borrows the way a credit-repair company turns a messy credit file into a clear, dispute-ready report—but here, the asset is a lender-ready income statement.

The solo operator collects the worker’s platform earning statements (Deliveroo, Uber, Just Eat, etc.) and bank feeds, applies a checklist built from real lender feedback, and produces a PDF pack with a plain‑English summary, a data‑formatting key for underwriters, and a supporting letter of explanation. The service costs £249 and takes five working days.

Why it works: UK mortgage lenders are notoriously bad at assessing multi‑app, variable gig income. Many brokers reject these applicants outright. A standardised, well‑argued pack removes the frictions that cause automatic declines. Over time, the operator builds a proprietary dataset of exactly what each lender’s portal demands—format, field names, evidence types—which compounds with every new case and lender update. That dataset is hard to replicate because incumbent brokers have no motive to share lender‑specific nitty‑gritty, and lender criteria change faster than public guidance can keep up.
