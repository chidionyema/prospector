AI customs audits for Savannah port importers.

An audit that reviews AI-generated HTS classification on port imports and flags errors before CBP issues a penalty.

The Port of Savannah is the fourth-largest container port in the United States, and many importers now run AI tools to classify incoming cargo under the Harmonized Tariff Schedule. CBP audits under 19 USC § 1592 carry penalties of up to four times the duty loss for negligent misclassification and substantially more for fraud. The licensed customs broker or trade compliance officer at a Savannah importer doesn't have a way to spot which AI classifier is systematically misclassifying which HTS chapter.

The service consumes the importer's AI classification output, runs it against CBP's CROSS ruling database and the importer's historical entry data, and delivers a pre-filing audit dossier flagging high-risk entries. Pricing is per shipment or per container bundle, with a retainer for importers running steady volume through Garden City or Ocean Terminal.

This borrows from how IRS tax-prep software audits flag errors before the taxpayer files, applying that pre-submission review discipline to customs. The private dataset of which AI classifiers systematically fail which HTS chapters only an auditor running many importers' flows can build, because CBP's CROSS database is a static ruling library, not an error-rate-by-tool working set.
