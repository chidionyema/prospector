MobiLift – The Carer’s Mobility Equipment Sale Broker.

A white‑glove resale service that sells the carer’s unused stairlifts, scooters and profiling beds directly to vetted refurbishers, guaranteeing a minimum price and handling collection, so the carer receives cash in days instead of months with zero hassle.

When a care‑at‑home relative passes away or moves into residential care, the primary carer is left with expensive, bulky equipment that they are emotionally and logistically unable to sell themselves (fragmented buyer market, no transport, unclear pricing). Existing online marketplaces are zero‑effort for the seller but yield low offers, while refurbishers have no systematic way to source second‑hand stock from individual homes. By acting as a transactional broker that inspects, prices, markets, negotiates, and arranges physical collection for a trailing commission (e.g. 25% of final sale price), the solo operator turns a sunk asset into immediate cash for the carer, capturing value from the structural friction that both sides cannot resolve alone. (Lens: invert – instead of the carer being a passive payer, they become a motivated seller arbitraging the value hidden in their home, while the broker’s income grows with enforcement of the disposal imperative.).

Primary carer (pays a percentage of the sale proceeds only when the equipment is sold – no upfront cost).

Rising equipment costs + squeezed middle carer finances + impending tighter waste electrical regulations make disposal a looming burden; AI‑based photo valuation and route‑optimised collection scheduling make it possible for one person to run a national logistics‑light operation.

Two passages state stairlifts do not hold value like traditional appliances and that resale value is usually low, with used units 'rarely retain[ing] more than a small fraction of their original price,' directly undercutting the claim that there is durable resale value to broker.
