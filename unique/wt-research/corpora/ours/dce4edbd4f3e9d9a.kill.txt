MotabilitySell — The carer’s fixed-fee Motability vehicle sale broker.

We sell your end-of-lease Motability car for a flat fee, handling buyer vetting, paperwork and the scheme’s strict resale rules so you keep the profit without the hassle.

A primary carer who holds a Motability lease on behalf of a disabled family member has the right to buy the car at a below-market price when the lease ends. Many carers never exercise this option because selling a car privately is daunting, and Motability’s rules forbid obvious commercial resale. MotabilitySell acts as a transaction broker: it takes over the entire sale process — buying the car from Motability in the carer’s name, listing it, vetting buyers and completing the DVLA transfer — for a fixed fee payable only on successful completion.

The primary carer pays a fixed fee of £495 upon completion of the sale. The carer receives the full net sale proceeds minus that fee and the original Motability purchase price.

Motability has recently tightened its guidance to discourage informal profiteering, making it harder for everyday carers to navigate the paperwork without professional help. Meanwhile, the second-hand car market remains buoyant, and charity awareness campaigns are prompting more carers to discover the purchase option. The complexity is increasing exactly when the financial upside is highest.
