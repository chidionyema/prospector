The Online Safety Act 2023 covers UK platforms with user-generated content. Ofcom's first Statement of Designated Codes (Illegal Harms Code, applying from 17 March 2025 for Category 1 services; Children's Safety Codes applying widely through 2025) requires in-scope platforms to run a Risk Assessment, document content moderation measures, and publish an annual Transparency Report; records must be kept for at least six years. A UK startup platform with user-generated content (an AI companion, a marketplace, a social app) falls in-scope at modest user thresholds.
