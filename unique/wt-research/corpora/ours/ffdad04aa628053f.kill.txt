ClientRisk Brief – the weekly email that rates UK companies on how reliably they pay freelancers, using court data and inside tips.

A weekly subscription briefing that scores a company's payment track record for freelance creatives, built from UK county court judgments and a private panel.

Freelancers lose thousands every year to clients who pay late or never, yet they have no systematic way to check a prospect's payment behaviour beyond a quick Google. ClientRisk Brief borrows the model of a corporate credit‑rating agency but turns it into a journalistic product for sole creatives. Each week, the email profiles two firms. It uses a composite score drawn from publicly available Companies House filings (like late penalty notices), Registry Trust county court judgment data, and confidential tip‑offs from a growing panel of freelance designers, writers and developers who log every invoice they chase.

The score is not a legal credit rating – that would need a licence – but an editorial risk assessment grounded in hard evidence. The solo operator writes the briefing and manages the data pipeline. Over time the panel's data set becomes a moat: it captures granular, real‑time payment patterns no free register can match.

Why it sticks: the more freelancers join the panel, the sharper the scores get, and the harder it is for any rival to recreate that proprietary data lake. The email arrives every Monday morning, priced at £9 a month, so a freelancer can check a new lead before they sign the contract.
