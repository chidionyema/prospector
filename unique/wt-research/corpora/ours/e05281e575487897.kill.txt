Council Tax Shield Data Brief — your personalised forecast of council tax reduction eligibility and appeal success, based on your local authority’s hidden decision patterns.

A data brief that reveals your real chances of getting a council tax reduction and how to win an appeal, built from FOI data on your council.

Many pensioners who live on modest incomes do not claim the council tax reduction they are entitled to. Others apply and get turned down when a different council would have said yes. This product pools FOI requests sent to every local authority in England and Wales, capturing anonymised decision‑level data: approval rates by income band, household composition, and disability status; average processing times; and the arguments that succeed at tribunal. The retiree enters a few details online. The report returns a personalised eligibility score, a comparison of how their council’s decisions stack up against neighbouring authorities, and a template for a tribunal appeal that cites the exact evidence types that have persuaded their council’s own review panels in the past. A single data‑gathering pass builds a moat that deepens automatically as new FOI returns come in, making the product a self‑compounding intelligence asset.

A homeowner aged 65–75 whose income has fallen after retirement but whose assets push them into a grey zone for automatic awards. They pay £39 for a report that can save £500–£1,500 a year.

High inflation and frozen pension credit thresholds mean more retirees fall into the ‘near‑eligible’ band where an adversarial, data‑led appeal makes the difference.
