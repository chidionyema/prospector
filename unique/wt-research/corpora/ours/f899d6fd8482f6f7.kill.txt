AI procurement dossiers for agencies selling to IL insurers.

When your agency pitches AI to an Illinois insurance carrier, a fixed-fee dossier proves you meet NAIC and IL DOI requirements, so procurement stops bouncing you.

Plain version: a productized dossier builder that turns an agency's AI pitch (claims triage, underwriting copilot, fraud detection) into the documentation package the National Association of Insurance Commissioners Model Bulletin on Use of AI by Insurers and the Illinois Department of Insurance's AI bulletin require, mapped to the specific procurement template the carrier uses.

Why it works: Illinois is the US insurance headquarters (State Farm in Bloomington, Allstate in Northbrook, Country Financial in Bloomington). The IL Department of Insurance adopted the NAIC Model Bulletin on AI in 2024, requiring every insurer to document AI governance before procurement. Agencies pitching AI to these carriers keep losing RFPs because their documentation does not match the format procurement committees grade on, and a Big Four consultancy charges $50,000-plus for a generic version.

Why it holds: borrows the FDA 510(k) substantial equivalence pattern, where a vendor must show their device is substantially equivalent to a previously accepted device on the dimensions the regulator cares about; the dossier does this for the agency's AI product on the dimensions the IL DOI bulletin cares about. The DOI bulletin itself is free, and Big Four decks are too expensive and too generic; the agency needs a fixed-fee, Illinois-specific, carrier-customized version it can re-use across Allstate, State Farm and Country Financial without rewriting.

Illinois-based dev agency owners (typically 5 to 20 people) selling AI features to insurance carriers, including the major IL-domiciled carriers and the larger regional carriers headquartered in Chicago, Peoria and Bloomington.
