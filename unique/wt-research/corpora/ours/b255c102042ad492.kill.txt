SuiteProof — The Independent Upholsterer's Per-Suite Fire-Retardancy & Fillings Compliance Brief.

A vertical web tool for UK 1-5 person upholstery workshops that ingests a fabric/foam bill-of-materials per suite and emits a Trading-Standards-ready FR-compliance brief, matched back-coating treatments and a per-batch traceability ledger.

First-principles rebuild from raw pain: a sole-trader upholsterer reupholstering a three-piece suite has ~14 days between fabric arrival and customer hand-back and cannot afford £180-£250 per-fabric BS 5852 lab tests, so they accept the supplier's own compliance claim on faith — which is exactly where Trading Standards is now prosecuting post-Grenfell under the Furniture (Fire Safety) Regulations 1988 (as amended). The tool ingests supplier SKU codes + their stated FR status, cross-references a proprietary dataset of independently-verified FR treatments, CMHR foam additives and banned-substance watch (PBDEs, decaBDE), and produces a per-suite verdict + remediation list (back-coat supplier, FR interliner spec, customer disclaimer clause) plus a 6-year retention job ledger for defence.

UK sole-trader and 2-5 person upholstery workshops reupholstering domestic three-piece suites — the ~3,800 VAT-registered workshops in the NAFAS / British Furniture Confederation / Worshipful Company of Upholders ecosystem who each do 40-120 suites a year at £900-£4,500 retail.

(1) Post-Grenfell Trading Standards enforcement on FR compliance has shifted from advisory to prosecutorial, with multiple high-profile upholsterer prosecutions in 2023-2024. (2) Post-Brexit, the supplier landscape churned: previously EU-domiciled fabric/foam now re-routes via NI or direct import, breaking old datasheet chains. (3) PBDE / OPFR foam additive bans are phasing in one-by-one through 2026, requiring continuous re-spec. (4) The defunct FIRA Gold list has not been refreshed since 2022, leaving a live data gap.
