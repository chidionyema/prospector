The Beekeeper's Yield Brief — Pollination Contracts, Honey Benchmarks & Colony Loss Epidemiology.

A paid monthly trade brief for US and EU commercial beekeepers that publishes crowdsourced almond-orchard pollination rate trends, regional honey wholesale price indices, and a subscriber-shared winter colony-loss dashboard.

Analogical transplant of Bloomberg commodity-trader briefs and AcreTrader agricultural-investor newsletters, fused with the Covid-19 wastewater-surveillance crowdsourcing model: ~15k US commercial beekeepers (plus several thousand EU/AUS equivalents) manage a multi-billion pollination-and-honey business on whisper-network intel. They have no specialist B2B media — Bee Culture and American Bee Journal are hobbyist-leaning and supplier-ad-funded (Piereco, Dadant, brushy-mountain-bee-farm), which structurally prevents them publishing pollination-fee or honey-wholesale benchmarks that would erode advertiser pricing power. A paid no-ads monthly brief that aggregates anonymised per-region colony-loss reports and pollination-contract strike-rates from its subscriber base becomes the operating dashboard consolidators, queen-genetics vendors, and equipment suppliers cannot ship without cannibalising their own pricing transparency.

US commercial beekeepers running 500+ colonies (most earn six-figure annual pollination contracts alone); EU apicultural association members.
