ClashBack — The Casual Worker's Universal Credit Pay-Cycle Audit & Backpay Pack.

A done-for-you productized service that audits a gig or casual worker's last 12 months of Universal Credit statements against their actual payroll calendar, finds the assessment-period clashes (two paydays landing in one UC month) that silently wiped out hundreds of pounds of their award, and delivers a ready-to-file mandatory reconsideration pack plus a forward pay-date plan to stop it recurring.

Lens: broaden. Instead of fixing one bad UC month (the narrow consensus shape), widen to the worker's entire annual pay-calendar value chain: weekly, fortnightly and 4-weekly paid casual workers structurally collide with UC's rigid monthly assessment periods, producing months where UC drops to zero and months of clawback — a defect the Court of Appeal (Johnson) already forced DWP to correct for monthly-paid claimants but which volatile-paid gig workers must still fight case-by-case. A solo operator with AI parsing of UC statement PDFs and payslips can industrialise this: detect every historical misallocation, generate the RTI-evidence-backed reconsideration letter for backdated correction, and issue a personalised 'pay-date defence calendar' (which shifts to accept, when to report, when a clash month is coming) as the recurring artefact. The compounding asset is a proprietary case archive of which decision-maker arguments and which employer payroll calendars (major hospitality, warehouse and delivery platforms) produce clashes and which reconsideration framings win — data DWP will never publish and generic advice charities never systematise.

18-27 year old casual and gig workers on Universal Credit with weekly/fortnightly/4-weekly pay (hospitality zero-hours staff, warehouse temps, delivery riders) — paying a fixed £39 audit fee, or a success-based £79 when the pack recovers a wiped month (typically £300-£900 of restored UC), plus a £5/month clash-forecast calendar subscription.
