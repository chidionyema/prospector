ResellLedger — the daily operating system for UK Vinted, Depop and eBay resellers.

A £7 a month app that shows the true profit on every flip, prices new stock from real UK sold prices, drafts buyer replies and stays ahead of HMRC.

Tens of thousands of UK 18-to-27-year-olds now run a reselling business on Vinted, Depop and eBay without realising it is one. They source from charity shops and boot sales, list across two or three apps, and only discover in January that HMRC expects a share of what they earned. The app they use every day is just the marketplace; nothing joins the three platforms into one margin-aware workflow, and no tool tells them whether that £4 charity shop jacket will actually clear £18 after Vinted's buyer protection fee, payment processing charges and postage.

ResellLedger is the daily command centre for this side-hustle. It pulls orders from Vinted, Depop and eBay through their public APIs and a screen-scraper for completed listings, so the seller sees one ledger of true profit per item, net of every fee. It uses a growing UK database of real sold prices (not asking prices) to suggest a listing figure when the seller photographs a new piece. It drafts polite replies to 'is this still available?' and 'lowest price?' pings. It watches the rolling 12-month gross and flags when the seller is approaching the £1,000 trading allowance ceiling, the £6,000 Class 2 National Insurance boundary, or a sudden income spike that would collapse their Universal Credit award.

The asset that compounds here is a cross-platform sold-price database no single marketplace exposes. Vinted shows only asking prices, Depop shows nothing, eBay shows recent sold prices but only on its own listings. A regulator's free Making Tax Digital tool cannot see a seller's true margins across three platforms, and a US-focused cross-lister like Vendoo has no UK tax logic and no UK sold-price coverage. Once a seller has used it for a season, their message templates, pricing memory and HMRC-ready ledger live inside it, which is what makes the product hard to leave.
