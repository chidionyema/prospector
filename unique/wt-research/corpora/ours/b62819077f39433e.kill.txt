LunchBox Lift – the teacher's monthly kit of ready-to-use classroom resources and self-care treats, delivered to your school.

A monthly subscription box for California public school teachers that delivers time-saving classroom materials and stress-relief items, available only to union members.

Teachers in California spend hours each week creating classroom materials and often use their own money. LunchBox Lift partners with the California Teachers Association (CTA) to offer a monthly subscription box curated with pre-made bulletin board kits, laminated teaching aids, and stress-relief items like hand cream and tea. The box arrives at the teacher’s school each month, with seasonal and curriculum-aligned themes. The exclusive distribution agreement with the CTA gives us access to over 300,000 potential subscribers through the union’s member-only online store and email list. This captive channel blocks competitors from reaching this audience directly. As members subscribe, we build a proprietary dataset of teacher preferences across districts, enabling better curation and personalisation over time. The business can be run by a small team handling sourcing, packing, and customer support, while the CTA handles promotion in exchange for a commission. This borrows the subscription-box model from beauty and pet niches and applies it to the overlooked market of public school teachers’ professional and personal wellbeing.

The CTA is actively looking for new member benefits to boost retention.
