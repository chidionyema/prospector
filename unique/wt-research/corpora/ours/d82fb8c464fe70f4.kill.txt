PayNudge — The tool that writes payment reminders proven to get you paid, using a network of California small businesses.

PayNudge connects to your accounting software and crafts invoice reminders that adapt based on what worked for similar California businesses, so you get paid faster without sounding like a robot.

Small business owners dread chasing late invoices. Generic dunning emails get ignored. But what if your reminder could say ‘93% of contractors in Los Angeles who sent this message on a Wednesday got paid within 4 days’? PayNudge does that. You connect QuickBooks or Xero, and it pulls overdue invoices. Instead of sending the same stale template, PayNudge builds a sequence that changes the wording, send time, and even the social-proof statistic—all tuned by the anonymized payment patterns of thousands of other users. Each reminder carries a subtle ‘Powered by PayNudge’ link; curious recipients can sign up for their own business, creating a viral loop. The product gets smarter with every user, because the data on what language, timing, and frequency yields settlement grows denser. This is a first-principles rebuild of the ‘dunning’ process—instead of automating existing workflows, it rebuilds the nudge from behavioral economics and network data, ignoring how it’s always been done. Retention is natural: a business owner who sees a 15% faster average payment isn’t going back to Outlook.

Small business owners in California—plumbers, freelancers, marketing studios, bakers, any 1-to-20-person shop who sends invoices and hates waiting 60 days for a check. They pay a monthly subscription (tiered by number of invoices). They feel the pain of late payments directly in their personal bank account and will try anything that costs less than the cash-flow gap.

US late payment rates spiked post-pandemic, and California’s small businesses are disproportionately affected because of high operating costs and the state’s strict debt-collection rules (Rosenthal Act). Electronic invoicing and open banking APIs are now ubiquitous, making it trivial to pull invoice data. Meanwhile, behavioral science has proven that social proof and temporal framing dramatically increase response rates—but no tool has productized this for B2B reminders at the small-business scale.
