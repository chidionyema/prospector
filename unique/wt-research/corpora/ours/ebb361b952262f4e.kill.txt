CPPA enforcement risk scoring for ad-tech privacy teams.

An AI-built library of every California Privacy Protection Agency enforcement, scored by similarity to your data practices, so ad-tech privacy teams spot the next action before it lands.

The California Privacy Protection Agency began publishing its own enforcement actions in 2024 and is escalating under its current leadership. Ad-tech companies with California audiences now staff at least one privacy analyst. That analyst's recurring chore is reading every new CPPA action and California Attorney General privacy settlement, comparing them to the company's data flows (real-time bidding, cookie syncs, attribution, device graphs), and updating the privacy program. Each new enforcement costs the analyst days of work plus a privacy review meeting.

This product reads every new CPPA action and AG settlement, extracts the specific practice that triggered the citation, the data type, the consumer segment, and the penalty, then scores each of the buyer's own data practices for similarity. The buyer logs in, sees a risk score by practice area, and reads a plain-English summary of past 'actions like yours' with their outcomes. Privacy teams use it before launching new data uses, during annual reviews, and after any regulator contact.

What compounds is the buyer-uploaded anonymized practice descriptions - every customer makes the similarity model smarter, and a copycat starting tomorrow has zero prior practice library. CPPA publishes actions on cppa.ca.gov, AG settlements on oag.ca.gov, and class-action filings on CourtListener. Existing privacy tools (OneTrust, TrustArc, BigID) sell workflow products but explicitly do not score risk against prior enforcement, and their threat-intel add-ons cover generic breach news, not CPPA specifically.
