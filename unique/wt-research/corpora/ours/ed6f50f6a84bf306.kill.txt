Electronics returns broker for ecommerce sellers.

An AI broker that finds the highest-paying compliant UK buyer for each returned electronic item, takes a cut of the recovery, and remits the rest to the seller.

UK ecommerce sellers returning electronics face two stacked problems: they must dispose of every item through a WEEE-compliant route, and they want to turn the item into cash rather than pay a recycler. Today these are dealt with separately: a WEEE compliance scheme (Valpak, Recolight) sells the seller a compliance certificate, and a separate bulk recycler takes the item for the price of scrap.

The broker does both at once and keeps the upside. A returns liquidation specialist used to grade items by hand, phone refurbishers to find a home for one-off batches, and book the haulage. The AI replaces that person at per-item granularity: vision models grade each phone, laptop, or headset from a photo; a database of authorised buyers is queried for the best price on that grade; the broker takes a 20% cut of whatever the buyer pays above the seller's current disposal baseline.

The compounding piece is the data. Every transaction yields a fresh price point per SKU, per grade, per condition, per buyer; per-buyer reliability (paying on time, not rejecting lots) builds a reputation score that is hard to copy from outside. Buyer-side network effects kick in once the broker processes enough volume — refurbishers want to be in the network because the alternative is paying someone else's margin. Switching cost on the seller side is the Shopify and Amazon FBA returns integration.

Distribution is product-led through the Shopify App Store and Amazon Seller Central partner directory, where returns apps already rank. The regulatory moat is the WEEE authorisation check: each buyer in the network must hold an Approved Authorised Treatment Facility (AATF) or Exempted Producer Compliance Scheme registration with the Environment Agency.

UK ecommerce sellers on Shopify, Amazon, and eBay that ship electronics — phones, laptops, headphones, small kitchen appliances — and have returns volumes of roughly 50 to 2,000 items a month. The buyer is the head of operations or the returns manager at a DTC electronics brand, with sign-off from the finance lead because the deal is commission-only and reduces a cost line.

Three signals converge. The Environment Agency has stepped up WEEE enforcement since 2023, with named-and-shamed non-compliant sellers and per-offence fines up to £5,000. Royal Mail's 2024 Returns Report puts ecommerce return rates at 25%, and electronics returns run higher. And the second-hand electronics market is large enough to be public: Back Market's UK growth disclosures and MusicMagpie's AIM-listing filings show the refurbisher side has capital and wants supply.
