Per-client records for UK mobile beauty therapists.

A per-client treatment, patch-test and consent record built for UK mobile nail techs, lash artists and brow therapists, so the insurer-grade audit trail writes itself.

Around 60,000 UK therapists earn their living travelling to clients for nails, lashes, brows and makeup. Every one of them pays £200 to £400 a year for treatment-risk cover through providers like Hiscox, Salon Gold and Professional Beauty Direct. When a client has a reaction, the insurer's first three questions are: what product did you use, what was the batch number, and when did you last patch-test them. The therapist today keeps this in Apple Notes, on the back of business cards, and in WhatsApp chats. When the claim lands, she cannot produce a record, and the claim is refused.

TopCoat is a vertical SaaS that gives each therapist a structured per-client file: patch-test photo with expiry date, product and batch number logged per visit, contraindications flagged, consent captured, and a one-click PDF export shaped to what the major insurers ask for. The therapist adds a client in 90 seconds during the appointment, and the app pings her when each regular is due for a re-patch (six months for lash adhesive, twelve for tint).

This borrows the structured-per-patient record used in veterinary EMRs and dental charts and applies it to a vertical that has nothing of the kind. The wedge is the data itself: each therapist's client database is unique, and after two or three years it is irreplaceable. A new therapist starts free for her first ten clients, then pays £9 a month or £79 a year. Distribution is product-led through VTCT-accredited training colleges (over 200 in the UK), co-marketing with the insurers themselves, and beauty wholesalers like Sally Beauty and Crown Supplies.
