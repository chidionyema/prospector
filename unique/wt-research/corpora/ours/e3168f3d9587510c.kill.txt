The Disabled Facilities Grant Approval Atlas.

A FOI-derived per-council data layer mapping every English local authority's Disabled Facilities Grant approval rate, mean award, decision time and stage-of-refusal, graded for self-funding retirees applying for ageing-in-place adaptations.

Retirees aged 65-80 advised by an OT to apply for a level-access shower, stairlift, ramp or wet-room under the Disabled Facilities Grant — a £10,000–£30,000 statutory entitlement — face wildly inconsistent council approval behaviour: between upper-tier LAs the variance runs 5% to 95%, refusals cluster at the means-test or OT-suitability stage, and councils almost never publish why. A proprietary per-LA compound — pulled by templated quarterly FOIs from all 152 English upper-tier councils, with intervention-type breakdown, decision-stage timing and refusal-reason classification — lets an applicant pick the right evidence wording, anticipate delays, and challenge refusals using their own LA's benchmark. One FOI cycle per quarter compounds over four years into the only navigable view of behaviour currently buried in unlinked council PDFs. Distribution is content-led via SEO + referral relationships with Home Improvement Agencies (HIAs) and OT channels. (Lens: narrow — one statutory entitlement, one cohort, one structural form.
