Disability Duty Refund Broker.

Audits your past overseas purchases of specialist disability equipment and reclaims incorrectly charged import duties that should have been exempt, splitting the refund 70/30.

Aggressive customs enforcement post-Brexit causes erroneous duty charges on goods entitled to relief; a solo operator can arbitrage the gap between HMRC's automated collections and the manual reclaim process, building a compounding database of decision patterns.

Primary carers (contingency fee from refunds obtained from HMRC).
