Rota Recall, gets your school's back-pay decision in writing.

For teachers and NHS staff whose employer quietly downgraded their pay grade, this fixed-fee service writes the formal grievance and the statutory pay-statement demand that forces a written answer within weeks.

A teacher or a nurse in their forties notices their payslip is missing a responsibility allowance, or that a TLR payment stopped when the timetable changed. They raise it verbally, HR says it will be looked at, and nothing happens for two terms. Chasing it costs an evening a week and feels like career suicide.

This is a fixed-fee writing service, not software and not a lawyer. You send your last twelve payslips, your contract, your job description and your timetable or rota. You get back three documents within five working days: a formal written grievance under the employer's own procedure, a written request for an itemised pay statement under section 8 of the Employment Rights Act 1996, and a schedule of loss showing exactly what is owed month by month with dates.

The angle is the reverse of the usual one. Most services help employers stay compliant. This one is built to get sharper the harder the employer pushes back, because every refusal creates a dated paper trail that a tribunal reads badly. Section 8 gives an employee the right to an itemised statement of pay, and section 11 lets them ask an employment tribunal to determine what should have been in it. A tribunal that finds unnotified deductions can order repayment of those deductions under section 12. The employer's own free HR portal will never write the letter that triggers that route, because the portal exists to defend the employer.

The operator is one person with a document set built from real school pay policies (the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document, published annually by the Department for Education) and NHS Agenda for Change terms. Every case fed back into the templates makes the next letter tighter: which trusts fold at grievance stage, which wording gets a Band 6 job description re-evaluated, which academy trusts ignore the first letter entirely. That case history is the part nobody can copy from a Google search.

Distribution is free and owned: staffroom Facebook groups, NHS staff-side reps, union branch newsletters, and a public library of anonymised outcomes. Price it at £180 per pack, with a £90 follow-up if the employer replies badly.
