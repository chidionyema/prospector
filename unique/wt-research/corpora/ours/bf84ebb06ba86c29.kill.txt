AccountKey CA — we unlock your parent's digital accounts after death or incapacity, for a flat fee.

For a flat fee per account, we send the legal requests and follow up until you get into your parent's online accounts.

When a parent dies or becomes incapacitated, the primary carer needs to access email, cloud storage, social media, and financial accounts to handle affairs. Each platform (Google, Apple, Facebook, banks) has its own opaque, shifting process for fiduciaries under California's RUFADAA (Probate Code §§ 870‑884). Doing it yourself means guessing which forms and evidence each company actually accepts, often getting rejected, and burning weeks of time a carer does not have.

This service acts as a transaction broker: the carer authorizes us as their agent with a power of attorney, and for a flat fee per account we prepare and submit the specific documentation that platform currently requires, handle follow‑up, and push until access is granted. It borrows the model of a public‑records requester, but applied to private digital assets, where the real “records” are the internal decision rules of each platform.

Because the platforms’ interests are to minimize data release, the rules change often and are not published. We build a proprietary database of what actually works — exact forms, notarized statements, and escalation paths — compounding with every request. That data moat makes the service stronger over time and structurally un‑copyable by someone starting fresh.
