The Retirement Village Exit Penalty Atlas.

A FOI + Land Registry + resale-scraped dataset tracking every UK retirement village development on resale losses, exit fees charged, ground-rent escalation history and freeholder/operator identity — sold as an interactive atlas plus £149 'due-diligence dossiers' on a specific village before a retired buyer (or adult child researching for one) commits.

(invert lens) The Leasehold Reform Act 2024 and intensified FCA scrutiny of retirement-housing sales are mandatorily surfacing more disclosure, but the regulator (DLUHC / FCA) structurally cannot publish a commercial 'do-not-buy' risk ranking nor allow an official body to take the consumer's side against an operator. As enforcement intensifies, the proprietary merging of Land Charges, FOI freeholder responses and Trade-in-resale price scrapes becomes a compounding dataset the operator cannot replicate and the regulator cannot ship.

An adult child of a retiree (the digital-native researcher buying for an elderly parent) and a retired person 60–75 themselves in digital-support mode, evaluating or actively purchasing a leasehold retirement property. Secondary buyer: solicitors doing pre-purchase due diligence on retirement leasehold.

Retirement village resale discounts of 20–50% are routinely reported but unindexed; Leasehold Reform Act 2024 is forcing freeholders to disclose escalation clauses and exit-fee structures; FCA increased its scrutiny on retirement-housing sales practices in 2024–25; no central 'retirement-village risk index' exists because the regulator cannot publish a commercial ranking.
