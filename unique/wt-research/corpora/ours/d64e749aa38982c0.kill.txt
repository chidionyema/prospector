OutgrowDesk — The Disabled Primary Carer's Specialist Home-Equipment Matched-Resale Desk.

A solo-operated consignment transaction broker that takes outgrown specialist disability equipment (specialist car seats, postural seating, bath hoists, standers, gait trainers, feeding chairs) from one primary-carer family, runs it through a proprietary clinical-suitability and condition-rating protocol, and matches it to another primary-carer family for a 20–25% consignment commission.

Primary carers of disabled children and adult-children caring for elderly/disabled relatives routinely pay £1,500–£10,000 for specialist home equipment that the cared-for person outgrows or graduates from within 12–36 months. Reuse is structurally undersupplied because: (a) NHS Community Equipment Service reissue is statutorily restricted to NHS-prescribed items for NHS-caseload families on capped Integrated Care Board budgets, (b) charities like Newlife and Caudwell Children have pivoted to grant-vouchers rather than trade-ins because taking consignment gear would compete with their own retail pipeline, and (c) eBay and specialist 'Special Needs Children UK Buy/Sell' Facebook groups offer opaque pricing, no suitability matching, and a low-trust handshake environment for £2,000+ items where clinical fit matters.
