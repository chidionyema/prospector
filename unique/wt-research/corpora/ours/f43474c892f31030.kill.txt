In-Car Data Liberation Agent for Independent Garages.

A solo service that extracts, normalises, and relays telematics data from connected cars to independent repair shops, enabling them to offer OEM-grade diagnostics and predictive maintenance without manufacturer support.

Under the EU Data Act, independent garages now legally own the right to request their customers' car data from OEMs, but they lack the technical ability to parse raw data streams and negotiate access against OEM friction. A solo agent can act as the technical and legal bridge—charging garages per vehicle per month—by building a library of API parsers and a standardised output format, all automatable via scripts. The wedge is proprietary data: each parser for a specific OEM model is a reusable asset that rivals must reverse-engineer from scratch, and the agent accumulates a unique database of access patterns and OEM resistance tactics that becomes harder to replicate over time.

Independent auto repair shops with 1-5 bays, serving 100-500 vehicles annually, typically in suburban or rural areas, who lose service revenue to franchised dealerships offering advanced diagnostics.

The EU Data Act application date (12 September 2025) has just passed, and the first disputes over access resistance are emerging. Garages are eager but helpless; no established player has filled this gap, and OEMs are actively throttling access, creating an acute pain no first-party tool will solve.
