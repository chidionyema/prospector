CarePlanAudit — the carer's fixed-fee care-plan audit and challenge pack.

An independent report that scrutinises your loved one's council care plan under the Care Act and demands the support they are legally owed.

When a local authority sets up a care plan for an adult with needs, it must meet the Care Act 2014's eligibility rules. Time-pressed carers often accept what they are given because challenging the council means navigating legal language, Ombudsman case law, and local assessment criteria. This service turns a carer's frustration into a structured, legally grounded audit. For a flat fee, the solo operator reviews the care plan and supporting documents, identifies specific breaches of the national eligibility framework or statutory guidance, and drafts a challenge letter that quotes the relevant regulations, local Ombudsman decisions, and the council's own published policies. The result is a pack the carer can send directly to the council to demand a reassessment or an improved package. It works because councils make mistakes under resource pressure, and a well-argued challenge often prompts a rethink before formal complaints. The operator builds a proprietary dataset of council response patterns and Ombudsman rulings to sharpen the templates over time, creating a moat that a simple template site cannot copy. This borrows the logic of the SEND tribunal appeal pack but applies it to adult social care, where no equivalent product exists yet.

A primary carer for an elderly or disabled adult living in England who has been offered a care package by their local authority and believes it falls short of what the law requires. They pay directly for the report to avoid the cost and delay of a solicitor.
