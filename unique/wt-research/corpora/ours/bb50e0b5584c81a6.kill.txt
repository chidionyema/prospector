StormBridge — The Tree Surgeon's Post-Storm Insurance-Claim Wage Bridge.

A risk-financing desk that advances up to 75% of the value of completed emergency storm-damage arboricultural work to self-employed tree surgeons within 48 hours, priced against a proprietary dataset of UK insurer claim-acceptance rates, settlement delays, and TPO-related dispute frequency by local planning authority.

Self-employed tree surgeons complete £2-15k of insured emergency storm work for householders but wait 60-120 days for the insurer to settle, while wages, chipper fuel, MEWP hire, and sub-contractor costs hit their cash NOW. Mainstream invoice factors (Bibby Financial Services, Hitachi Capital, MarketInvoice) categorically exclude insurance-pending claims because the debtor (the insurer) has not yet admitted liability — the invoice is contingent, not a receivable in law. By pricing each advance against a continuously-updated statistical model of acceptance probability per (insurer × work-type × local authority), the operator can underwrite a thin risk edge no bank or general factor will accept, and price it at 8-12% of the advanced amount for an 18-25% IRR on a 60-90 day exposure.

The UK arborist workforce is shrinking as TIC-qualified climbers retire faster than they are replaced, so surviving operators are doing larger jobs with thinner cash reserves. Insurers are simultaneously tightening TPO/Conservation Area validation, producing more disputed claims that need expert working-capital support rather than blunt factoring.
