CareFund Check — the carer's tool that finds every error in the council's care contribution calculation.

A self-service tool that scans your relative's financial assessment and generates a challenge letter with the correct legal disregards.

When a loved one moves into care, the local authority completes a financial assessment to set the contribution. These calculations are dense and error-prone — carers acting as appointees frequently miss disability-related expenditure disregards, tariff income miscalculations, or the incorrect application of the upper capital limit. This web app takes the assessed figures and the person's circumstances, then cross-references them against a proprietary database of each council's charging policies and the statutory disregards in the Care and Support (Charging and Assessment of Resources) Regulations 2014. It instantly flags every mistake and produces a pre‑written challenge letter citing the relevant law. The tool grows stronger with every appeal outcome fed back, compounding a dataset of where each council regularly under‑assesses. This borrows the adversarial intelligence of a consumer refund tool, and applies it to the means‑test where the duty‑imposer — the council — structurally cannot give carers an independent checking tool.
