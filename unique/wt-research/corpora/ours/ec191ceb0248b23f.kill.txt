SettlementScope — the self-represented divorcée's financial settlement proposal builder.

A tool that shows you what a fair split of assets looks like under UK divorce law, built from thousands of real court outcomes, and turns your numbers into a judge‑ready financial proposal.

When a marriage ends and neither side can afford a lawyer, most people walk into mediation with no idea what a court would give them. They guess. They get bullied. They leave money on the table.

This tool lets you input your finances — house equity, pensions, savings, debts, incomes — and instantly see the range of outcomes judges actually award in cases like yours. The numbers come from a growing proprietary library of anonymised financial remedy orders obtained through Freedom of Information requests to family courts across England and Wales.

It then generates a Form E‑compatible financial statement and a position paper you can take to mediation or a first hearing. You pay a fixed fee per household.

The value compounds as more people use it, because every new case that is added (with consent) sharpens the predictions. No competitor can get that dataset, because it’s built through sustained FOI campaigns and manual parsing of scanned court orders — a legal but laborious moat a solo operator can own and defend with a small script once the data is digitised.
