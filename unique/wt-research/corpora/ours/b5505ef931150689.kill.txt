FeeBack — the delivery rider’s missing pay recovery service.

Scans your Deliveroo and Uber Eats earnings for underpayments from app glitches, then gets the money back.

FeeBack is a fixed-fee service that audits a delivery rider’s past earnings statements from platforms like Deliveroo and Uber Eats. Riders upload their weekly payment breakdowns and provide a log of trips they took. FeeBack’s engine cross-checks for common errors: cancelled orders that weren’t paid correctly, miscalculated distance fees, missing boost or surge payments, and duplicate deductions. It then generates a ready-to-submit dispute pack with evidence tailored to each platform’s claims process. The rider pays a flat fee of £5 per recovered £20, only if the service gets money back.

This borrows the way medical claims auditors comb through hospital bills line by line to recover overcharges—an approach that works because the payer (the platform) has opaque, bug-prone systems and the rider lacks the time to argue every small error. As the service accumulates data on recurring glitches and effective arguments, it compiles a proprietary playbook that makes claims faster and harder for platforms to reject. That dataset is the moat—no competitor can copy the pattern-matching heuristics without processing thousands of claims.

The service operates purely online, uses OCR and template engines to automate most of the work, and can be run by a single person with AI assistance. Distribution is through social media channels popular with UK delivery riders, such as TikTok and Reddit communities, where riders share tips on maximising earnings.
