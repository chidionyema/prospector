Life insurance broker for elderly owners and caregivers.

A fixed-fee transaction broker that shops a primary carer’s parent’s life insurance policy to a network of vetted buyers and secures the best cash price.

Primary carers of elderly parents often hold life insurance policies that are no longer needed or affordable. Selling the policy for cash (a life settlement) can unlock funds for care, but the market is a tangled mess of phone calls and opaque offers. PolicyCash steps in as a skilled broker. The carer uploads the policy details once, our system solicits binding offers from a wide network of institutional buyers, and we present only the top offer, ensuring full transparency. The carer pays nothing upfront; we take a flat 5% of the final sale price only when the deal closes. All paperwork is handled digitally, and the entire process is audited by state insurance regulators. This borrows the model of real estate agencies, where the agent’s incentive is aligned with getting the best price.

The primary carer—or the policy owner—pays from the settlement proceeds at closing. They feel the acute pain of time poverty and financial pressure, and a life insurance policy can be a hidden asset they never knew they could monetise.

The US life settlement market is over $15 billion annually and growing as baby boomers age. Yet most transactions happen through high-commission brokers. With new state-level transparency regulations (like California’s SB 984 requiring clear disclosures) and the rise of digital aggregation, there’s a window to build a carer-centric platform that undercuts incumbents and uses data to compound its advantage.
