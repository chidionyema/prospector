DeputyCheck – The Lay Deputy's OPG Audit Shield.

A web app that pre‑screens a lay deputy’s OPG annual report using a predictive model trained on FOI‑sourced regional audit outcomes, flags risky expense categories, and auto‑generates a rejection‑proof narrative pack to cut audit risk by 60% and save hours of stress.

Lay deputies dread the OPG’s adversarial annual accounting, where a single flagged expense can trigger months of evidence requests. Free OPG guidance is reactive and generic, solicitors cost £500+. By building a classifier on historical FOI audit data per region, we can predict exactly which expense narratives fail scrutiny and automatically assemble a defensively worded report—transplanting the data‑driven dossier model to the deputyship domain.
