CareFee Lens – the self-funder’s window into what your council really pays for care.

See your local authority’s secret care home rates and use them to negotiate your own fees down to the council’s price.

When a local authority pays for someone’s care, it negotiates a weekly rate with care homes that is typically 30-40% lower than the “self-funder” rate. Many retirees paying for their own care don’t know this. This tool inverts the council’s information advantage by using FOI requests to compile a database of block-contract rates for every care home in a given area. A retiree or their family enters the postcode and care home name, and the tool shows the council rate for that home, if available, and provides a script and letter to ask the home to match it. The dataset is proprietary and constantly updated — the council cannot stop gathering and sharing it, but they won’t volunteer it. The moat is the aggregation work; a solo operator can run it with automated FOI submissions and scraping.

Retirees (or their adult children) facing self-funded care home fees. They pay £79 for a localised report, including a negotiation guide.

Care costs are soaring, and the “self-funder subsidy” is under growing scrutiny. The Competition and Markets Authority has noted the gap, but no consumer tool yet exists.

Free league tables already publish local-authority care rates gathered by FOI across all 173 English and Welsh councils, including residential homes, and current fee-rate maps continue that coverage. That free public dataset removes the paid tool’s claimed durable edge in aggregating and selling council rates obtained the same way.
