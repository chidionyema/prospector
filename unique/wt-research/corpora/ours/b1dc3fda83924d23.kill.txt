The NHS Resolution Case Value Atlas.

A FOI-sourced, case-level database of NHS Resolution (formerly NHSLA) clinical-negligence settlements and judgements, indexed by Trust, harm type, procedure and age band — sold as a per-case 'comparable settlement' report so an affected retiree (or adult child acting for one) can see what similar NHS incidents at the same Trust have actually paid out, with percentile ranges.

(invert lens) NHS Resolution publishes annual aggregate tables but cannot ship a filterable case-level valuation engine, and FOI is the only mechanism that surfaces per-Trust, per-harm-type forensic detail. As NHS complaints climb and NHS Resolution's own reporting becomes more aggregated/defensive, the value of this proprietary, FOI-harvested case library compounds. NHS Constitution amendments expanding duty of candour further surface data points the regulator cannot aggregate itself.

An affected retiree 60–75 (or more often their adult child in a digital-support role) who has experienced harm from NHS treatment and is weighing a formal claim or complaint. Secondary buyer: small solicitor firms running clinical-negligence matters who buy one-off comparable-settlement reports as case-prep.

NHS complaint volume is at a post-pandemic peak, NHS Resolution has been restructured and publishes less, FOI request pipelines are mature enough that systematic, Trust-by-Trust case-level harvesting is viable, and adult-child proxy claimants for elderly parents are now the dominant pre-solicitor 'trier' of medical-harm cases.
