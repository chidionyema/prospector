PA workers' comp hearing binder for legal software.

An AI that turns PA workers' comp hearing transcripts into a labeled exhibit binder with the 90-day appeal window already mapped onto each issue.

PA's Workers' Compensation Appeal Board (WCAB) hears disputed claims at 60+ district offices, with a 90-day window to file appeals and a strict exhibit-labeling convention (medical records by defendant, deposition transcripts by date, independent medical exam reports separately). Defense counsel for PA workers' comp carriers spend paralegal hours preparing a labeled hearing binder from the medical and deposition file — work that consumers of WC legal practice software expect to be embedded in the platform.

Today, paralegal-helping AI tools (e.g., generic summarization models) don't know PA WCAB conventions. Generic AI summarization produces a working summary but not a binder that the WCAB judge will accept. The free path is ProLaw or Needles plus the WCAB's own packet template — neither automates the 90-day deadline map or the exhibit labeling per the WCAB convention.

This borrows the way medical AI scribers (Abridge, Nuance DAX) summarize doctor-patient conversations and surface discussion items, and applies it to PA WCAB hearing transcripts. The tool turns the carrier's case file into a PA-WCAB-compliant binder: exhibits labeled per the WCAB convention, every issue tagged with the 90-day deadline, and a one-page hearing brief drafted from the deposition cross-examination. The WC legal software vendor pays a flat fee per case prep. Why this holds: the AI is tuned against a corpus of decided PA WCAB hearings that a generic chat model cannot replicate.
