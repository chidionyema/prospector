AccrueBack — The Casual Worker's Unpaid Holiday Pay Recovery Pack.

A fixed-fee productized service that turns a zero-hours or agency worker's messy payslips into a fully quantified unpaid-holiday-pay demand pack — accrual ledger, statutory calculation, employer demand letter, ACAS early-conciliation form and tribunal-ready schedule of loss — across ALL their casual jobs at once.

Lens: broaden. Instead of the consensus shape (a per-employer dispute tool), widen to the worker's whole casualised value chain: a Gen Z worker typically stacks 2-4 income sources (staffing-agency shifts, hospitality zero-hours, event work, app gigs reclassified as workers post-Uber v Aslam), and EACH accrues 12.07% statutory holiday pay that is chronically unpaid or rolled up unlawfully. The daily friction is concrete: they see gross pay per shift but have no idea thousands of pounds of accrued leave exists, and no single employer view ever reveals it. The productized service ingests payslips/rotas from every source, computes the accrual under the post-Harpur-Trust and 2024 Employment Rights (Amendment) rules per engagement type, and ships a done-for-you recovery pack per employer plus one consolidated 'what you're owed' ledger. Priced as a small fixed fee per pack (£35) plus an optional uplift on recovered sums — sized for a tight monthly budget because the pack pays for itself on the first recovered week of leave. Solo-operable: payslip parsing, quantum calculation and letter generation are fully automatable; the operator's judgment is only in edge-case classification. The moat compounds: every case adds to a proprietary dataset of which agencies and hospitality groups settle, at what percentage, after which escalation step — making each subsequent pack's demand letter calibrated in a way no fresh copier or DIY worker can match.
