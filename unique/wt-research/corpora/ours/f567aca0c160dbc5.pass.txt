PCI scope packs for UK ecommerce agencies.

An AI-built scope report that tells a UK ecommerce agency which PCI DSS controls they owe on a given Shopify, Stripe or WooCommerce build.

PCI DSS v4.0 became mandatory on 31 March 2025 with two new e-commerce controls (6.4.3 for payment-page script authorisation and 11.6.1 for payment-page script integrity monitoring) that any UK agency building checkout on Shopify Plus, Stripe Connect, Adyen or WooCommerce has to handle. Each new build triggers a decision the agency cannot avoid: which Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ) applies, which compensating controls are owed, and what the merchant's acquirer (Worldpay, Barclaycard, Elavon) will demand in writing before signing off the merchant.

A productized service that, for a fixed fee, takes the agency's architecture brief (platform, payment service provider, hosted fields versus custom fields, iframe versus redirect, MOTO versus recurring versus crypto) and returns within five working days a written scope decision naming the SAQ, the named compensating controls, and a one-page merchant letter, would replace the £3,000 to £8,000 Qualified Security Assessor scoping call that agencies either absorb or push back on the merchant.

The compounding asset is a corpus of scope decisions against named UK stacks (Shopify Plus Checkout Extensibility, Stripe Connect with custom charges, Adyen for Platforms, WooCommerce Subscriptions with Stripe). Each engagement makes the next faster and aligns the language to what UK acquirers accept.

The first-party alternative is the PCI Security Standards Council's own SAQ selection wizard, which returns a generic SAQ code without the agency's specific stack architecture. Vanta and Drata are built for the audited-entity use case (continuous control monitoring), not one-off deliverable scoping, and neither carries a corpus of named UK platform stacks with the merchant letter language UK acquirers demand.
