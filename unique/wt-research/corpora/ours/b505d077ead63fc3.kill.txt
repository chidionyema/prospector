DBS Update Service signals for UK HR software vendors.

Reads the DBS Update Service every day for continuous-vetting changes per UK employee, and serves the score to HR software vendors to embed.

The plain version: an HR software vendor serving UK employers with regulated-role workforces (financial advisers, care workers, education staff) subscribes to a daily feed that watches the DBS Update Service and surfaces a per-employee risk-change flag inside the HR vendor's own dashboard.

Why it works: the DBS Update Service lets subscribers (more than two million as of the November 2023 published figures) keep their DBS certificate portable, with employers carrying out instant checks. HR SaaS vendors currently rebuild status checks from scratch via UCheck, Onfido or HireRight. The product reads the same Update Service portal across all participating employees of all participating customers, normalises status across Basic, Standard and Enhanced-with-barring-list certificates, and serves an embedded change flag to the HR vendor's customer UI. The buyer is the product or operations manager at a UK HR SaaS selling into employers with regulated staff.

Why it holds: UCheck, Onfido, HireRight sell direct to the employer and are not positioned to be an embedded multi-tenant API for HR SaaS vendors. Each HR vendor individually is too small to justify building the integration. Serving many HR vendors at once produces the wedge.
