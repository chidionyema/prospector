FlipWise – The Solo House Flipper’s Maximum Offer Engine.

A browser-based calculator that pulls local sold comps and renovation-cost data to instantly tell a part-time property flipper the highest price they can pay for a distressed property and still make a 15% net margin.

First-principles rebuild of the house-flipper’s spreadsheet: the core pain is not finding deals but confidently computing the maximum allowable offer (MAO) when renovation costs are guesswork. Stripped of broker hype, a flipper needs a defensible number that accounts for hard money costs, holding period, and ARV. By building a proprietary database of trade-specific, localized reno costs—sourced from public building-permit data, material price feeds, and user-submitted actuals—this tool out-calculates free calculators and embeds itself into the flipper’s pre-offer workflow.

The squeezed-middle professional who flips 1–2 houses a year as a side hustle, paying a $29/month subscription or $49 per deal analysis because a single mispriced offer could lose them $20k.
