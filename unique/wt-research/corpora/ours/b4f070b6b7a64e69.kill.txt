VapeCycle Rounds — disposable vape waste collected from your shop so you don’t get fined.

A mobile service that collects spent disposable vapes from independent vape shops and ensures they are recycled legally.

Every vape shop in the UK disposes of hundreds of used disposable vapes each month. These are waste electricals (WEEE) and cannot go in general rubbish. New DEFRA and Environment Agency rules require separate collection and recycling, with fines of up to £5,000. Most small shop owners have no easy way to comply.

VapeCycle Rounds runs a weekly or fortnightly collection run — like a modern milk round for vape waste. The operator visits shops, picks up a sealed bin of used vapes, weighs it, and issues a waste transfer note. The load goes to a licensed WEEE recycler. The shop pays a flat fee per collection, covering everything. The operator makes money from the difference between collection fees and the recycling cost, plus route density.

This borrows the clinical-waste collection model used by GP surgeries and puts it into the vape sector. The founder owns the customer relationship and the repeat schedule. The wedge is thin, but being first in a town builds trust and makes it more hassle for a shop to switch than to renew. A solo operator needs a small van, a waste carrier’s licence, an app for routing and invoicing, and a relationship with a local recycler. Starting with ten shops in one town and growing by word of mouth and leafleting is realistic.

Independent vape shop owners and corner shops that sell disposable vapes. They pay a monthly subscription of £30–£50 per collection. They are the ones getting warning letters from environmental health officers and want a simple, fixed-cost way to stay compliant.

From January 2025, the UK’s updated WEEE regulations force separate collection of waste electricals. Disposable vapes are a priority because of the volume of waste. The Environment Agency is stepping up enforcement, and councils are informing retailers. Shops can no longer legally put them in the general bin, and there is no ready-made collection service for small independents.

UK commercial ads solicit quotes for paid vape recycling, bins, and collections nationwide, and GAP offers to set up licensed store collection with transfer paperwork—so retailers are already being sold paid fixes for this waste.
