PartyPressure Pack — The Squeezed-Middle Birthday Cost Decoder.

A workbook that takes your child's birthday week, postcode, and guest-list size and produces a per-venue cost breakdown plus a printable 'we're keeping it small this year' card to hand to relatives — built on a proprietary compiled dataset of UK party venues, entertainers, and cake prices by postcode.

Squeezed-middle parents feel acute party pressure around ages 5-11: each year the cost compounds (venues, entertainers, return gifts, party bags, cake cutting fees) with no neutral source compiling live per-postcode pricing. The current 'free' alternative is parent-forum threads — anecdote, not audit. By transplanting the procurement-rate-card pattern from B2B to the household, a workbook with proprietary venue + entertainer + cake price data plus a printable 'no-thanks' script converts a yearly dread into a 30-minute defensible decision. Lens: analogical (B2B RFP cost-transparency playbooks → family-event budgeting).

Parents aged 35-55 with primary-age children, household income £45-90k, booking 1-3 birthday parties per year and wanting a defensible case against peer escalation. Payer is the named budget-authority parent (typically the mother).
