EscortEdge — The Primary Carer's Clinical-Appointment Void Broker.

A real-time void-fill broker that matches UK primary carers needing short-notice clinical-appointment companionship (hospital discharge, oncology review, care-needs assessment, SEN tribunal) with pre-vetted companions whose NMC/HCPC/SWB registration, DBS status and specialty tags have been live-verified, taking a 30% cut per matched engagement.

Employed UK primary carers aged 35–60 (the squeezed-middle cohort caring for elderly parents or disabled adult children) routinely face the impossible schedule of attending a clinical appointment WHILE someone competent is with the person they care for. NHS, council brokerage and care agencies structurally cannot fill this: NHS Volunteer Responders won't verify an NMC pin, council Direct Payments require a 6–12 week social-services assessment, and care-agency unit economics force a £400+ monthly package rather than a £45 point engagement. The broker vets companions against the live NMC/HCPC/Santé register APIs, tags them by clinical specialty and postcodes, then MATCHES each void (companion's spare 2–4 hour window) to a specific primary-carer need in real time. Each match compounds the proprietary vetted-roster dataset — specialty × postcode × appointment-type × reliability rating — which neither general sitter marketplaces (Bark, Sittercity) nor incumbent volunteers can replicate. Monetisation: primary carer pays £45 per matched engagement, broker keeps ~£13.
