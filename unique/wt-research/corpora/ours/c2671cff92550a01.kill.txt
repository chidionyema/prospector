GigPay Reclaim – the gig worker’s fixed-fee service that gets your unpaid holiday pay and minimum wage.

A fixed-fee service for UK delivery and rideshare gig workers to claim back wages and holiday pay owed after worker-status rulings.

Gig platforms like Uber, Deliveroo, and Just Eat often treat their couriers and drivers as self-employed contractors. But UK courts and tribunals have repeatedly ruled that many of these people are actually workers with a legal right to holiday pay and the national minimum wage. (The trade calls this worker status.)

Most couriers don’t claim what they’re owed because the process is complex and they’re busy working. This service changes that. For a fixed fee — or a percentage of what you get back, whichever you prefer — we calculate the exact amount your platform should have paid you, write the legal claim, and handle the back-and-forth until you’re paid.

It works because we’ve mapped the winning legal arguments from cases like Uber v Aslam and put them into a repeatable, platform-specific process. A small team of paralegals can run it for hundreds of workers at once. The law is on your side; we just get you the money.

A gig worker who has been treated as self-employed and missed out on holiday pay or minimum wage. They pay either an upfront fixed fee from £99 or a 25% success fee from the recovered amount – whichever the worker chooses.
