The Derelict Dock Diver: Marina Asset Recovery Intel.

A weekly subscriber-only video and dossier service for marina operators and marine insurers that identifies abandoned, derelict, or illegally moored vessels in non-tidal UK waterways, using publicly available satellite imagery and local harbour master notices, sold as a competitive intelligence and risk mitigation tool.

Marinas and marine insurers face growing liability and berth-blocking costs from abandoned boats, but lack the capacity to manually monitor their patch. By cross-sector borrowing the 'asset recovery intel' model from the commercial shipping and salvage industry (e.g., Lloyd's weekly casualty reports), a solo operator can curate and sell a focused, niche intelligence feed to a captive audience of 200-300 UK marina managers and marine insurers who will pay £150-300/month for a time-saving, actionable early-warning system that reduces their cleanup and legal costs.
