PondGuard – The Predictive Aquaculture Sentinel.

A vertical IoT+AI tool for small-scale fish and shrimp farmers that ingests low-cost sensor data, predicts lethal oxygen crashes hours in advance, and dispatches actionable alerts to prevent catastrophic stock loss.

Existing aquaculture monitoring tools are either industrial-grade and unaffordable or simple alarm boxes that beep after a threshold is breached. A first-principles rebuild treats pond dynamics as a tiny time-series forecasting problem, fusing dissolved-oxygen, temperature, and pH data with weather forecasts and stocking density. A solo operator can ship an app that connects to commodity sensors (e.g., Atlas Scientific) via a Raspberry Pi bridge, runs a lightweight predictive model, and alerts the farmer via SMS before the crash. The moat is the species- and region-specific prediction model that improves with each pond it monitors.

Owner-operators of small to medium aquaculture operations (ponds or raceways, 1–10 employees) who face a 30–100% mortality risk from sudden water quality collapses.

Aquaculture is one of the fastest-growing food sectors, yet smallholders are massively underserved by AgTech because the unit economics require a low-cost, self-install solution. Cheap single-board computers, cloud IoT hubs, and edge ML (TinyML) now allow a solo builder to deliver a predictive tool without needing a hardware venture.
