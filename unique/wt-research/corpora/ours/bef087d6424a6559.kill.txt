The Property Tax Appeal Evidence Builder.

A web tool that equips US homeowners to fight assessment increases by generating a county-compliant comparable-sales evidence packet and appeal letter, using proprietary local market data and argument templates.

Homeowners are over-assessed but lack the time or expertise to compile a persuasive appeal; the county provides raw data but no advocacy. By building a data engine that localizes comps and past appeal success patterns, a solo operator creates a tool that increases win probability for a flat fee.

US homeowners aged 35–55 with recent assessment increases and assessed values above market, motivated by a potential tax saving of $1,000+.

Post-pandemic home price volatility has made assessment errors rampant, while many counties are under-resourced, creating a window for DIY appeal tools.
