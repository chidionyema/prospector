PlatformAlpha: The Freelancer's Gig Discovery Letter.

A paid monthly newsletter that identifies, rates, and timestamps early-stage online platforms where freelance writers and designers can land high-value, low-competition gigs before they become saturated.

Transplanting the Motley Fool stock-pick model, this gives freelancers a systematic edge in platform selection, akin to investors timing market entry for maximum return.

Freelance writers, designers, and developers who actively hunt new client sources and are willing to pay for actionable, vetted intelligence that saves them from dead-end platforms.

The gig-platform ecosystem is extremely volatile—new platforms launch weekly, early movers earn premium rates, and latecomers face fee compression. AI now enables a solo operator to scrape, analyse, and rank platforms at scale, delivering quantitative scores and a decisive 'early entry' signal that no free source replicates.
