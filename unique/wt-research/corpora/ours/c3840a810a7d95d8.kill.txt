FixedHours Claim — the zero-hours worker's tool that turns your shifts into a permanent contract legally.

A self-serve web app that tracks your agency work pattern and automatically generates a binding request for a fixed-hours contract under UK law.

Many young workers on zero-hour contracts with agencies don't know that after 26 weeks they can request a fixed work pattern, and the agency must respond reasonably. This tool makes it easy: the worker inputs their shift history (or syncs a calendar), and the app calculates eligibility under the Agency Workers Regulations 2010 and the Good Work Plan. It then generates a formal letter to the agency with the precise statutory wording, and if ignored, a complaint template to the Employment Agency Standards Inspectorate. The tool is self-serve, charging £19 per request. It encodes legal expertise that a typical worker would spend hours researching or paying a solicitor for.

The individual agency worker (gen_z_worker) pays directly for the tool, because it helps them move from unpredictable hours to stable earnings.

The UK government has strengthened agency worker rights recently, and there is growing public scrutiny on insecure work, but awareness of these specific entitlements is low. The tool capitalises on this gap, using a repeatable SaaS model to automate a previously manual legal process.
