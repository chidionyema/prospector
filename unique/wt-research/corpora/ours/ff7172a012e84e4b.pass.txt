AI red-team dossiers for Georgia defense vendors.

An independent review that converts an AI red-team report into a CMMC and NIST AI RMF-ready dossier for defense customers in Georgia.

Georgia hosts a deep defense ecosystem: Lockheed Martin in Marietta, Robins Air Force Base in Warner Robins, the Army Cyber Center of Excellence in Augusta, and a cluster of defense AI vendors bidding on prime contracts. When defense primes buy AI tools from smaller vendors, they require third-party validation against CMMC 2.0 and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. Smaller vendors run red-team tests, then discover that a raw red-team report is not what a prime procurement officer actually accepts.

The service turns the raw red-team output into the structured dossier a prime buyer expects: model inventory, control mapping to CMMC and NIST 800-171, AI risk profile, residual-risk rating, and remediation status. Pricing is per red-team report, with a retainer for vendors running multiple models.

This borrows from how OSHA industrial hygiene consultants turn ambient air measurements into compliance reports, applying that translating-a-raw-measurement-into-a-structured-document discipline to AI risk. Each engagement adds to a private library of how each AI model class fails each CMMC control, a working set the prime's own procurement team cannot replicate internally without standing up a permanent AI risk desk.
