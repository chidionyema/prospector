TailLight Rounds — The Shift-Worker's Evening Pet-Welfare Round.

A solo mobile evening round serving NHS, police and fire staff who worry about pets left alone during 12-hour shifts, providing dog walks, medication, fresh food/water and a pet-log update per visit for £18/visit or £120/month subscription.

Mobile evening pet welfare round run by one solo operator with a van, visiting 6-10 homes of NHS/police/fire staff per evening between 19:00 and 01:00. Each visit is a structured 30-minute pet welfare session: dog walk, food/water refresh, scheduled oral medication, brief welfare check, and written pet-log update sent to owner. Subscription model targets shift workers with acute guilt about pet welfare during long shifts. Distribution is free through NHS/police staff Facebook groups, hospital and station noticeboards, and ward-to-ward word of mouth.
