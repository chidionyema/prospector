The Public Bid Autopsy.

Freelancers lose bids because they guess what clients want; by reverse-engineering real winning proposals obtained through automated FOIA requests, they can adopt proven pitch structures, scope language, and rate benchmarks that are otherwise invisible. This creates a proprietary intelligence asset that compounds as more proposals are analysed.
