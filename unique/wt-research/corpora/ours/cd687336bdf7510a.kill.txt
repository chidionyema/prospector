California employee-monitoring notice for HR software.

For HR software vendors, a fixed-price service that delivers a California Labor Code § 435 employee-monitoring notice, acknowledgment, and posting per customer.

California Labor Code § 435 requires every California employer to give employees advance written notice of any electronic monitoring of their activities, with a signed acknowledgment and a posted workplace notice. The notice must describe 'the types of electronic monitoring the employer conducts'. Violations are misdemeanors (§ 435(d)) and have supported private claims since 2020; the California Labor Commissioner has pursued class claims on behalf of employees who never received the required notice.
