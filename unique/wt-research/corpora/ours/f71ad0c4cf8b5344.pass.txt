Prefilled chemical safety assessments for nail technicians.

A downloadable set of pre-filled COSHH assessments for the common chemicals in nail services, so a mobile nail tech can show the inspector and get on with her day.

A nail technician working alone from home or visiting clients must keep a file of Control of Substances Hazardous to Health assessments for every product she uses — the monomer, the acetone, the primers, the gel polishes. If the local council environmental health officer asks to see them and they are not there, she can be shut down. Most nail techs know they need these papers but have no time to read safety data sheets and write out one form after another, so they put it off.

This pack transplants the way a fitness trainer sells a generic diet plan that still feels personal, and applies it to workplace safety: a one-off purchase of a folder full of pre-written assessments that cover the 40 most-used nail products, already filled out with the proper hazard statements, exposure controls, and emergency procedures, ready to print and slot into a ring binder. The buyer just adds her name and date.

An individual solo operator can create this pack by collating publicly available safety data sheets from manufacturers’ websites, extracting the legally required information, and rewriting the assessments in plain English that matches a mobile beauty environment (working at a client’s kitchen table, not a salon). The work is done once; updates are published yearly when product formulations change, giving a reason for buyers to return. It is sold as a PDF via a simple website or on a platform like Etsy, with marketing in the nail tech Facebook groups where the need is openly discussed.
