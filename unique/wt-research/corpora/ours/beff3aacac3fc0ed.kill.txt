RouteFault — The Driving Instructor's Test-Centre Readiness Engine.

A shovel for independent driving instructors: a proprietary, per-test-centre fault-and-route dataset that scores each pupil's true readiness before the DVSA does, so instructors stop burning goodwill on premature bookings.

First-principles: strip away 'how do I teach' and the instructor's daily raw pain is a scarce, non-refundable test slot booked 4-5 months out against a pupil who may not be ready — a wrong call costs the pupil £62 and the instructor a furious parent and a wasted lesson block. The 'Why Now' change is the post-pandemic test backlog making slots a rationed commodity where mis-booking is uniquely expensive. I do not teach and I do not book: I sell the pick-and-shovel diagnostic input. Each instructor logs pupil lesson faults into my tool; in return they get a readiness probability calibrated to THEIR local test centre's specific examiner fault-weighting and known route hazards — data DVSA aggregates away. The corpus compounds: every logged lesson from every subscribing instructor sharpens the per-centre model, so a late copier starts from zero local data. Lens: first_principles.
