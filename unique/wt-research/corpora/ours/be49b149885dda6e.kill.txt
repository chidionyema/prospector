The business proposition relies on individual NHS staff purchasing premium, non-essential organizational gear out-of-pocket. Evidence confirms that NHS staff are already under significant financial distress, with many using credit or savings for basic living costs, and that they are already burdened by essential work-related expenses like professional registration fees, stethoscopes, and work shoes. Marketing luxury, non-essential stationery to this demographic is fundamentally misaligned; the Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) for individual sales will exceed the slim margins achievable on stationery, making the unit economics unsustainable.
