EstateExit — sell your parent's home, keep the money, and beat California's Medi-Cal estate recovery.

A commission-based real estate broker that sells a primary carer's parent's home while shielding the proceeds from California's Medi-Cal estate recovery, using a little-known caregiver exemption.

Primary carers in California often face a devastating choice when a parent on Medi-Cal passes away: the home is claimed by the state's estate recovery program to repay care costs. But under Welfare and Institutions Code § 14009.5, if the adult child lived in the home and provided care for two years, the home's value is exempt—yet few know this or can execute it. EstateExit acts as a transaction broker, simultaneously listing and selling the house and filing the exemption paperwork with the California Department of Health Care Services (DHCS). The sale proceeds go to the family, not the state, and the broker earns a standard commission (e.g., 5%) on the sale price. 

This borrows from the aggressive posture of distressed asset buyers, but inverts it: instead of buying the asset at a discount, we help the family liquidate it fully, taking a broker fee, while using the state's own exemption against its recovery unit. As DHCS ramps up enforcement (recently targeting over 10,000 estates per year), demand for a friction-free path grows. The state structurally cannot offer a service to sidestep its own recovery, and typical real estate agents lack the legal expertise to navigate the exemption. This creates a durable wedge of technical IP—a repeatable, document-driven process that compounds with each closed case and builds proprietary data on DHCS's approval patterns. Distribution is through partnerships with elder law attorneys, adult day care centers, and carer support groups across California.
