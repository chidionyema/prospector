The Retired Trade Pro's Gear & Gripes Podcast.

A weekly podcast and YouTube channel where a solo operator interviews retired construction tradespeople (plumbers, electricians, carpenters) about 'the one tool that never worked' and 'the one job that taught them everything,' monetized via tool brand sponsorships and a premium tier for early access and community chat.

Analogical transfer: The 'behind-the-scenes' interview format popularized by podcasts like 'The Bill Simmons Podcast' (sports) and 'WTF with Marc Maron' (comedy) can be transplanted to the skilled trades, where retirees have an immense store of uncaptured tacit knowledge, deep nostalgia for their craft, and a built-in audience of younger tradespeople and DIY enthusiasts. The audience is a captive niche with a high degree of shared pain (bad tools, bad materials, bad customers, bad contractors). The solo operator's moat is proprietary access to a network of vetted, engaging retirees and a growing library of irreplaceable stories that create deep audience loyalty and switching cost.

Tool, hardware, and workwear brands (e.g., Milwaukee, DeWalt, Wera, Carhartt) seeking highly targeted, authentic endorsements to reach a skeptical, craft-focused demographic; plus a small number of premium subscribers (tradespeople, apprentices, DIYers) paying $5/month for ad-free episodes, a private Discord, and first-look sponsor deals.
