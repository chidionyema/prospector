Defect work orders for UK boat operators.

Turn each plain defect note into a prioritised work order, so a small UK boat operator can give the engineer a usable job first time.

This is an AI-native tool. The model does part of a marine maintenance manager's paid work: it reads a note sent from the boat, extracts the vessel, equipment, symptom, possible safety consequence, and urgency, then drafts a work order with questions, likely spares, and the right person to check it.

The founder-operator, skipper-owner, or operations manager forwards a note and pays £15 for each structured job. Each accepted order builds a private history of that operator's equipment names and past faults. This broad approach gives a one-boat operator the defect-triage practice used by larger marine fleets. The model never signs off the repair.
