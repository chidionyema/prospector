The Probate Inventory & Valuation Toolkit.

A mobile app for probate clearance professionals that uses camera and AI to automatically catalogue household contents, assign defensible resale or charity‑donation values, and generate a formal inventory report ready for executors.

Solo probate clearance operators spend days manually listing and pricing chattels for estate administration, a repetitive task that delays settlements and limits the number of jobs they can handle. An AI‑powered tool that scans a room, identifies items, scrapes real‑time sold prices from eBay/Sotheby’s/etc., and produces an HMRC‑ready inventory drastically cuts this time while giving the clearance professional a higher‑value service to sell.

Probate clearance firms and independent house‑clearance professionals (mostly solo operators with 1–2 employees).

Object recognition is mature; pricing APIs are abundant; an ageing population is driving a steady increase in probate work; executors increasingly demand transparent, formal inventories, raising the standard from a handwritten list.
