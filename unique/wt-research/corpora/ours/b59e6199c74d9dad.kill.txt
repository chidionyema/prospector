The Compliance Narrator: Private Prosecution Watch.

A subscription newsletter and podcast that tracks and explains private prosecutions brought by companies against individuals (e.g., for IP theft, breach of contract, non-compete), monetised via individual subscriber fees and corporate sponsorship for 'case explainers'.

Analogical transfer: True crime podcasts have proven huge audiences for legal narratives. The audience is: legal professionals, business owners, and the general public fascinated by corporate power dynamics. Monetisation: $10/month subscriber model for deep dives + $5k/episode sponsorships from litigation funders or law firms who want visibility in this niche.
