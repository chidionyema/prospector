Enterprise security pack for UK AI agent vendors.

Fixed-fee document bundle that gets a UK AI agent vendor past enterprise CISO security review, with quarterly updates.

UK AI agent vendors — companies that build autonomous software which acts on data and calls other systems on a user's behalf — hit the same wall when they pitch to a UK enterprise: the customer's CISO asks seven questions in writing. What tools can this agent access? What data can it touch? What guardrails exist on its outputs? How is human oversight done? How are actions audited? What is the rollback plan? What is the prompt injection defence? Most vendors cannot answer in a structured, audit-ready form. They lose the deal, or they hire a Big 4 consultancy for £150k and six months.

This service productises the answer as a fixed-fee pack of seven documents, generated from a templated process, then maintained quarterly as the agent evolves. The first engagement costs £8–15k and takes four to six weeks; quarterly updates are £2–4k.

The pack draws on the public anchors enterprise CISOs already use to grade AI vendors: the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Articles 9, 14 and 15), the UK Information Commissioner's Office AI auditing framework, the Financial Conduct Authority's SS1/23 model risk management guidance for financial-services buyers, and ISO/IEC 42001 for AI management systems. Citing those documents is not optional — enterprise procurement teams increasingly score vendor responses against them.

The wedge compounds across engagements. Every pack we generate teaches us what enterprise CISOs actually accept, what wording closes the deal, and what guardrails they reject. That benchmark data is not published anywhere and gets sharper with every customer. A first-time entrant has to learn it the hard way; a vendor working with us skips that.

The growth motion is partnerships with the AI agent frameworks (LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen, OpenAI Assistants, Vertex AI Agents) for visibility at the moment a UK team ships its first agent product, plus targeted outreach to UK AI vendors at Series A funding events — the point where they start enterprise sales and hit this exact problem. Conversion is high because the alternative, a six-figure consultancy engagement, is unaffordable for a typical ten-person, £5m-raised vendor.
