SnagVault — The New-Build Self-Inspection Toolkit.

A guided mobile app that walks a new-build buyer through a 280-item room-by-room inspection on completion day, producing a contractor-grade snag report with AR-tagged photos, contractor-specific auto-letters, and a developer-response tracker.

New-build buyers in the UK pay £250-£800 for professional snagging inspections on completion day, but for a £400k-£800k purchase most can self-inspect if given a structured, room-by-room checklist with AR photo capture. A solo-built mobile app with a proprietary snag taxonomy, photo evidence templates, and auto-generated contractor letters removes the need for a paid inspection in 70% of cases and produces a stronger evidence pack than human inspectors in the rest. £29 one-off or £79 with developer portal upload and 12-week response tracking.

UK first-time buyers and families upsizing into new-build homes (£350k-£900k), typically aged 30-55 with mortgage pressure — the squeezed-middle in their single biggest financial transaction. Also leaseholders taking handover of new-build flats in London and Manchester.
