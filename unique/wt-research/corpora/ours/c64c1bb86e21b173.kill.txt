DoLS Defence Kit — The step-by-step binder that helps families challenge a care home detention order.

A physical binder with templates, guides, and a case law USB that helps the family of a care home resident understand and challenge a Deprivation of Liberty Safeguards (DoLS) authorisation.

When a care home restricts a resident's freedom, it must obtain a DoLS authorisation from the local authority. Families often feel powerless, unaware of the legal grounds for objection or the short 21‑day window to challenge. This borrows the 'legal self‑help kit' model used by US publishers like Nolo Press, packaging the relevant English law, template letters to the supervisory body, a plain‑English guide, and a logbook for recording concerns — all in a single purchase. It sells for £49.99 through a simple website, search‑optimised for queries like 'DoLS appeal help' or 'care home detention rights'. The kit is compiled and shipped by one person, with printed materials inserted into a branded binder and a USB stick pre‑loaded with case summaries.

Family members of a care home resident in England or Wales. They have just learned that a DoLS authorisation has been granted or is being applied for. They are motivated, often desperate, and willing to pay for a clear, immediate course of action.

The Liberty Protection Safeguards (LPS) were due to replace DoLS in 2022, but the government has repeatedly delayed implementation, most recently to 2024–25. In the meantime DoLS remains the live framework. COVID‑19 lockdowns exposed widespread unlawful detention in care homes, and media coverage has driven public awareness. Families are actively searching for ways to challenge, right when the system is under unprecedented strain and local authorities are slow to respond to informal queries.
