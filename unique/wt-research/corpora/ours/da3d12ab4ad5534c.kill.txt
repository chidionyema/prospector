PharmClaim – The Independent Pharmacy Reimbursement Arbitrage Tool.

A SaaS tool that connects to a pharmacy's management system, detects NHS prescription reimbursement underpayments, and generates a claim pack with evidence and clinical justification to recover the difference—automatically.

Small pharmacy owners lose thousands annually because they lack time to manually cross-reference complex Drug Tariff rules and wholesale invoices. A solo operator can build this tool leveraging a proprietary database of successful claim patterns and automate appeals.

Independent community pharmacy owners in the UK (1–5 locations, owner-operator).
