DewPoint Dossier Rounds — The Public-Sector Homeowner's Post-Retrofit Insulation-Failure Evidence Round.

A one-person instrumented survey round that visits pre-1980 homes of NHS, school and council staff after subsidised ECO4/GBIS insulation installs, logs 14 days of humidity, dew-point and wall-moisture data with left-behind sensors plus thermal-camera and borescope inspection, and converts it into a guarantee-agency-grade claim dossier that forces the installer's 25-year guarantee body to fund remediation.

Lens: combine_signals — fusing (a) the 2022-2026 wave of millions of subsidised cavity, loft and spray-foam installs under ECO4/GB Insulation Scheme, (b) Awaab's Law making damp-and-mould a household alarm word, and (c) lenders refusing mortgages on spray-foamed homes. A meaningful fraction of rushed subsidised installs cause condensation, cold-bridging and mould, but the homeowner faces an installer who stonewalls and a TrustMark/guarantee route that rejects claims lacking measured evidence. A solo operator with £2k of instruments (data-logging hygrometers, pinless moisture meter, thermal camera, borescope) runs a fixed geographic round: install loggers on visit one, collect and inspect on visit two, and auto-generate a dossier formatted to the specific guarantee body's rejection criteria. Every survey compounds a private per-installer, per-house-archetype, per-measure failure-pattern dataset that tells the operator in advance which claims will win, which house types to warn off, and which installers' work fails — data no rival or guarantee body will assemble because it only exists adversarially, on the homeowner's side.

NHS nurses, teachers and local-government staff aged 30-60 who own pre-1980 terraced or semi-detached homes that received ECO4/GBIS-funded cavity-wall, loft or spray-foam insulation and now have condensation, mould, or a mortgage/remortgage refusal — paying a £265 fixed fee per evidence dossier plus £95 for a post-remediation verification re-test; word-of-mouth spreads through staffrooms, ward WhatsApp groups and union noticeboards (owned/free distribution).

ECO4 and the Great British Insulation Scheme pushed record volumes of subsidised retrofits through 2022-2026 with widely reported workmanship failures; Ofgem and TrustMark have publicly acknowledged large-scale remediation of botched solid-wall and cavity installs; spray-foam lofts are now a named mortgage-lending exclusion; and Awaab's Law (extended in phases from 2025) has made measured damp evidence culturally legible to ordinary households — so demand, guarantee-funded payouts, and homeowner willingness to pay for adversarial measurement all peak simultaneously.
