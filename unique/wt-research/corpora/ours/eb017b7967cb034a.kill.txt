The Calibration Credit Line.

A parametric risk contract that instantly pays a tradesperson when a certified calibration check fails on their critical safety device, covering replacement and liability exposure.

Gas engineers, electricians, and refrigeration techs depend on calibrated instruments, but a single failure can halt work and expose them to fines. Borrowing the parametric trigger model from natural-disaster bonds, a solo operator designs a product where a digital calibration report from an accredited lab triggers a predefined cash payout—no claims adjustment, no delay.

The manual tradesperson (gas engineer, electrician, etc.) pays a small recurring premium or per-test fee.

Safety regulators are tightening calibration mandates (e.g. UK Gas Safe rules), and the cost of replacement tools plus potential liability makes a broken device a critical business-continuity risk.
