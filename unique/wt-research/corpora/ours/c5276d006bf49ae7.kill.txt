InspectAppeal – The Restaurant Health Code Defense Engine.

A web tool that transforms a restaurant owner's violation details into a data-backed appeal dossier, using FOIA-obtained outcomes from similar infractions to maximize reversal odds.

Independent restaurant owners receiving health code violations often feel powerless to appeal due to opaque processes and lack of legal knowledge. There is a friction in translating raw violation data into a persuasive, precedent-driven argument. A vertical_tool that combines a structured interview with a proprietary dataset of past appeal decisions (obtained via FOIA) can craft a statistically optimized appeal letter and evidence checklist, turning a panic event into a rule-based rebuttal.

Simultaneously, open-records laws and natural language processing make it feasible for a solo operator to compile and analyze thousands of anonymized appeal outcomes across jurisdictions, providing a data moat that didn't exist five years ago.
