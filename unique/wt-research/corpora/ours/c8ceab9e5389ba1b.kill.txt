AI copyright binder for California agencies.

A fixed-fee binder that documents human authorship of your agency's AI-generated content, so California clients register the copyright and accept your IP terms.

An agency owner who delivers AI-generated marketing content to enterprise California clients is increasingly asked for two things they cannot produce: a copyright registration that names a human author, and an indemnification clause that survives a NYT-style training-data lawsuit. Today they pay an IP lawyer $500 an hour to draft both, and most small agencies cannot afford it.

We package the work into a 15-day fixed-fee binder. The deliverable is a structured PDF with three sections: a per-asset provenance record (what the human wrote, what the model generated, how it was edited), a copyright registration filing the US Copyright Office will accept, and an indemnification clause that enterprise legal teams have already reviewed. We charge $5,000 per binder, plus $500 per additional asset. The buyer is the owner of a small California agency (3-25 staff, marketing or design focus) signing or renewing an MSA with a Fortune 1000 client.

Stability AI are public reference points that enterprise legal teams already cite. The WPP, ANA and 4A's trade bodies have all published AI content guidelines that reference these same sources. The agency's legal team does not have a standard playbook; we do.

Why it holds: We accumulate a growing library of provenance records and indemnification language that enterprise legal teams have signed off on. Each binder makes the next one faster to draft and harder to displace. Big Law IP practices cannot productise a $5,000 fixed fee because their cost structure requires $25,000+ matters.
