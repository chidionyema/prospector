CareCostCompass — The Squeezed-Middle Eldercare Forward Planner.

A web app that turns a family's specific parental finances — savings, property, gifting history, local authority — into a 5–20 year projection of council-funded vs self-funded social-care costs, flagging deprivation-of-asset triggers and the inheritance-tax endgame.

Audience-specific friction: the squeezed middle is one care crisis away from losing £30k–£100k+ of inheritance to Local Authority means-testing, but has zero personalised, on-demand visibility into how the Care Act 2014, their specific local authority's published care tariffs, property-disregard rules and deprivation-of-asset case law interact over the next 5–20 years — they discover this only in crisis, when the gifting window has closed. Form: solo-built SaaS calculator that ingests the family's balance sheet, overlays a proprietary time-series dataset of all 152 English (plus Welsh / Scottish / NI) local-authority care tariffs and a curated deprivation-of-asset case-law library, and outputs a personalised PDF projection with concrete gifting, deferred-payment-agreement and property-disposal recommendations. Creativity lens (broaden): extends the category out of commoditised care-home selection (Carehome.co.uk, Elder) into the broader forward planning of sandwich-generation wealth transfer — a virgin territory with no commercial incumbent. The tool is paid £80–£250 per scenario run because the modelled decisions are worth 5–6 figures of preserved inheritance per family.

UK squeezed-middle adults aged 40–65 with at least one living parent aged 70+, in or near the 'sandwich generation' (mortgage + dependent kids + living parents) — exactly the segment forecast to absorb the bulk of the £8–9bn/year UK adult-social-care self-funding gap. Budget authority is personal, jointly held with spouse; an £80–£250 projection is trivial against a six-figure parental-care bill they are currently planning blind.

(1) English local-authority social-care budgets have been contracting since 2022, pushing more families onto self-funded tariffs — self-funded adult social care in the UK now exceeds £9bn/year. (2) The 2024 Autumn Statement set a £1bn+ cut to local-authority social-care funding, accelerating means-testing aggressiveness. (3) Property wealth held by UK over-70s averages £300k+, making deprivation-of-asset cases a 5–6 figure reality for sandwich-generation families — case volumes have risen ~30% since 2020. (4) Existing commercial offerings (Carehome.co.uk, Elder, Age UK's free calculator, the GOV.UK eligibility portal) all stop at selection or eligibility — none produces a forward, personalised, multi-decade projection that models LA-specific tariffs, gifting trajectories and property-disregard timing. The gap is precisely what a solo operator with a structured LA-tariff dataset and an AI-leveraged calculator layer can fill.
