G-Cloud bid loss analysis for UK govtech ops managers.

We fuse FTS award notices, CCS framework score sheets and the vendor's own bid history into a per-framework win-rate and scoring pattern read.

A per-framework scorecard for UK GovTech ops managers that fuses FTS award history, CCS framework scoring criteria, the vendor's own bid records and the published framework refresh schedules, ranking each framework by the vendor's pattern of scoring gaps and the competitors' track record.

An ops manager at a UK SaaS company selling to government through G-Cloud, the Digital Marketplace, or Crown Commercial Service frameworks loses bids constantly without knowing why. The Framework Agreement award notices on the Find a Tender Service (FTS) show who won and at what price. The CCS framework score sheets show the weighting criteria. The vendor's own bid history shows what they bid and how they scored. The ops manager has to read all three and figure out which framework they'll win next. The buyer is the ops manager or bid manager at a UK GovTech SaaS with 5-30 people; the deliverable is a per-framework readiness read with concrete gap-fix suggestions.

This widens the data the ops manager reads from a single register to the structured scoring pattern across frameworks. The lasting advantage is the dataset: the cross-referenced FTS awards + CCS score sheets + the vendor's own bid history grows with each new framework refresh and no competitor can replicate the per-vendor scoring pattern. FTS is free but raw, and the vendor's own bid history is private to the vendor.
