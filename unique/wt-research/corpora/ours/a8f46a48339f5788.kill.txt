Texas AEDT pre-interview notice generator for HR tech vendors.

An AI service that turns an employer's job description and AI screening tool into a TWC Rule §805.1-compliant pre-interview notice, for HR tech vendors and Texas employers who need it.

Texas Labor Code §21.0545 and TWC Rule §805.1 require any Texas employer using an automated employment decision tool (AEDT) to screen candidates to provide a written notice to each applicant before the tool is used. The notice must identify the tool, describe its criteria, and state whether it has a disparate impact. Non-compliance exposes the employer to TWC complaints, investigation, and penalties — and rejected applicants have a private right of action.

The rule is live now, but most HR teams and their software vendors have no idea it exists. HR tech vendors who embed AI screening into their products (applicant tracking systems, assessment platforms, video interview tools) are the entity responsible for ensuring the notice goes out — yet they have no tool to generate it. They currently either skip it (illegal), copy-paste a generic clause (not Texas-specific, not TWC-compliant), or pay employment counsel $300–500 per client onboarding to draft one from scratch.

This service takes three inputs — employer name, job title/description, and AI screening tool name and criteria — and produces a TWC Rule §805.1-compliant pre-interview notice ready to send. It also produces a consumer-facing privacy notice addendum required under TDPSA. A monthly subscription covers unlimited employer clients for the HR tech vendor; direct employers pay per notice or $299/month.

The durable wedge is a template library built against TWC enforcement patterns and specifically keyed to the rule's exact language requirements — a competitor starting from scratch cannot replicate the cumulative pattern-matching without running the same enforcement edge-cases through the same analysis. The workflow is fully automatable with current AI tooling: structured input → prompt with rule text → jurisdiction-specific output.
