SundownSight – spot the hidden patterns behind dementia behaviours before they happen.

A phone tool for dementia carers that logs daily episodes and blends them with local weather, light levels, and routine data to predict and reduce sundowning outbursts.

A mobile app that turns a carer’s daily notes into a behaviour map. The carer taps in what happened (agitation, wandering, confusion) and what the person ate, did, and was exposed to. The app automatically pulls local sunset time, weather, and indoor light readings from the phone’s ambient sensor. It then finds which combinations tend to trigger difficult days and shows the carer a simple forecast: this evening is high risk unless you try a different routine.

Primary carers looking after someone with mid‑stage or later dementia, typically spending out of their own pocket. They pay a monthly subscription of around £6 for the premium version that includes the prediction engine and shareable weekly reports for the GP or memory clinic.

Dementia diagnoses are rising fast across the UK, and the NHS Long Term Plan asks family carers to take on more non‑pharmacological management. At the same time, phone sensors and cheap API weather data are now good enough to give a personal micro‑climate. Carers are also more comfortable with self‑tracking apps, and we can piggyback on that behaviour without asking them to learn a complex medical tool.
