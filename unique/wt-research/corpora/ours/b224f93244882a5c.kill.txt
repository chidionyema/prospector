The Section 21 Flaw Finder.

A solo-operated service that turns a tenant's Section 21 eviction notice into a detailed invalidity challenge by scanning for hidden legal errors using a proprietary database of defect patterns and case law.

lens: first_principles. Friction: Gen Z renters face sudden no-fault eviction with 2 months' notice, but many Section 21 notices are invalid due to unaffixed prescribed forms, missing deposit certificate, or defective EPCs/gas safety records. External change: post‑COVID eviction surge + mandatory possession reform leaving loopholes. The service rebuilds from the tenant's pain, ignoring free advice leaflets, and delivers an actionable invalidation letter that forces the landlord to restart the entire process, buying months of housing stability.

Gen Z gig workers and students privately renting in England/Wales, receiving a Section 21 notice with less than 2 months to vacate.
