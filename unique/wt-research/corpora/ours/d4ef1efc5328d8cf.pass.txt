Stolen tool claim handling for California tradespeople.

A fixed-fee service that handles stolen tool insurance claims for California tradespeople from police report to payout at $295.

A plumber’s van gets broken into and power tools worth $5,000 disappear. Their insurer knows they can delay and lowball because the plumber lacks the time and knowledge to fight. ToolClaim Pro steps in as a dedicated claim advocate. We borrow the public adjuster model from large commercial claims but adapt it for small tool thefts — charging a flat fee instead of a percentage of the payout. The plumber uploads the police report and a tool list through a web portal. Our system automatically generates a comprehensive claim package with replacement-cost documentation, files it with the insurer, and then pursues escalation, from bad-faith demand letters citing California Insurance Code § 790.03(h) to complaints with the California Department of Insurance. Because we aggregate data across hundreds of claims, we know which carriers routinely underpay, how they respond to specific evidence, and what triggers a quick settlement. This proprietary dataset makes our advocacy more effective over time, creating a compounding moat that new entrants cannot replicate. The service is highly automatable: document assembly, statutory deadline tracking, and insurer communication templates handle most of the workload, allowing a team of three (a claims manager, a paralegal, and a developer) to manage hundreds of active claims.

Self-employed plumbers, electricians, carpenters, and HVAC technicians in California who hold commercial inland marine policies or tool floaters. They pay the $295 fee directly — out of pocket but willingly — because recovering $5,000 or more in tools is well worth the cost. These tradespeople have budget authority over their business expenses and feel the pain of idle time acutely.

Tool theft from work vehicles has surged in California cities like Los Angeles and San Francisco, yet police clearance rates are low and insurers continue to slow-walk small commercial claims. At the same time, California’s Proposition 103 and recent Department of Insurance enforcement actions have raised the stakes for unfair claims practices, giving professional advocacy more leverage than ever before. A tradesperson who gets their tools back in weeks instead of months can start earning again sooner, making the fixed fee a no-brainer.
