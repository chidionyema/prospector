UK high-rise residential buildings (HRBs, 18m+) must submit a safety case to the Building Safety Regulator (BSR, part of HSE) at each Gateway under the Building Safety Act 2022. The first Gateway submissions have been in scope since October 2023. BSR has begun publishing decisions, and many of them are rejections citing weak evidence in fire risk assessment, structural integrity, lifts, evacuation drills, and the 'golden thread' of building information. Each Gateway submission takes social housing landlords 12+ weeks to assemble, and rejections trigger a 6-month remediation cycle. Every UK HRB must now have a named Building Safety Manager.

The wedge is proprietary data. Every published BSR decision teaches the model which evidence gets rejected. BSR does not publish a fix-it list, and CAFM systems (PlanRadar, Ascora, Glider) do not grade evidence against published decisions. We start with the ~600 UK HRBs owned by social housing landlords (Peabody, Clarion, L&Q, Hyde, A2Dominion), then expand to private HRB owners and managing agents. The product broadens from the per-Gateway pack to the full UK HRB safety management pipeline, including ongoing compliance, remediation, and resident engagement.
