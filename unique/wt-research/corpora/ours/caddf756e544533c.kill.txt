Skill Escrow, banks unspent NHS study leave for later.

A fixed-fee service that converts an NHS or council worker's expiring training budget into a booked, funded qualification before the financial year wipes it out.

Every NHS trust, school and council allocates staff a personal training or study leave budget. It is use-it-or-lose-it. On 31 March the unspent money vanishes back to the employer, and the worker gets nothing.

Staff lose this money constantly. A ward nurse cannot get study leave approved because the rota is short. A council planner never finds a course that matches the internal course code the finance system will accept. A teacher's CPD allocation goes unclaimed because the request form needed a line manager signature in February.

Skill Escrow is a paid service, not software. For a fixed fee of about £180 the worker sends us their payslip band, their employer, and their goal. We find the accredited course their specific employer's policy will fund, we write the business case in the exact language that policy uses, we get the purchase order raised before the year-end cut-off, and we book a delivery date that fits their actual rota. If the claim fails, they pay nothing.

This borrows the way ticket brokers work a use-it-or-lose-it airline voucher: the value is not in the course, it is in knowing which of hundreds of employer-specific rules unlocks the money and when the internal deadline really falls. NHS Education for England, Health Education England legacy funding streams, apprenticeship levy transfers under the Finance Act 2016, and each trust's own study leave policy all have different tests.

The durable part is the policy library. Every claim we run teaches us one more employer's real approval path, real named approver role, real deadline, and real refusal reasons. After a few hundred claims across NHS trusts, multi-academy trusts and county councils we hold a body of employer-by-employer approval knowledge that no course provider and no employer has, because each employer only sees itself and each provider only sees its own bookings.

A founding team can raise on this. Distribution is paid social into nursing, teaching and local government Facebook groups in January and February, plus partner deals with the accredited providers who want the bookings. The service is largely a document-generation workflow, so one operator can run many claims a week.

Apprenticeship levy transfer work is the natural expansion: large employers must spend levy funds within 24 months or lose them to HMRC, and the same policy library maps who inside each employer can move that money.

NHS band 5 to band 7 clinical staff, teachers in multi-academy trusts, and local government officers with a personal training allocation they have not spent. They pay the fixed fee themselves, out of household money, because the qualification raises their band or opens a side income.

NHS and council pay is capped while training budgets are ring-fenced and separately accounted, so the money exists even where pay rises do not. The apprenticeship levy rules under the Finance Act 2016 give employers a hard 24-month expiry on unspent funds, and the March financial year-end forces a scramble every single year. Workforce shortages also mean line managers now block study leave on rota grounds more often than on cost grounds, which is exactly the objection a well-written business case answers.
