CareCredit Recoup.

Recovers unused prepaid care package fees from local authorities when a care arrangement ends, taking a commission only on successful refunds.

When a care home resident dies or moves, councils often retain weeks of pre-commissioned care fees that are refundable but buried under bureaucratic inertia. A solo-operated broker can use FOI data to map council refund-handling patterns and automate the claim-back process, monetizing an asymmetry that volunteer-sector helpers cannot match with a contingent model.

The carer or executor of the estate pays 20% of the recovered amount as a success fee.

Austerity-hit local authorities deliberately slow-track refunds to protect cash flow; meanwhile, rising care costs push families to recoup every penny. The combination of tight household budgets and council intransigence creates a window for a niche, data-driven recovery agent.
