FundedHours Gen — the childminder's termly funding claim pack, pre-filled for your council.

Turn your attendance data into a ready-to-submit council funding claim for the Early Years Entitlement, in minutes.

Registered childminders must claim early education funding (15/30 hours) from their local authority each term. Each of the 150+ English councils has its own form, portal, and evidence requirements—a fragmented mess that costs childminders hours of unpaid admin. This tool asks for your postcode and the children's details, pulls the right template and rules from a proprietary dataset of council-specific requirements, and pre-fills the claim form with calculations that meet the local authority's exact audit expectations. It produces a pack that the childminder uploads or posts, with a checklist of any supporting documents needed. The moat is the council-by-council intelligence, built from FOI requests, council policy documents, and user-contributed feedback on what each authority actually accepts. This borrows the way tax-preparation software tailors itself to different state forms in the US, brought to a UK market where no such thing exists for early years funding.
