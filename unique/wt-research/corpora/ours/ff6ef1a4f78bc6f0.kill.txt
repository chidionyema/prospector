The Padel Court Referee Booking Engine.

A vertical SaaS for padel clubs that replaces the ad-hoc, manual process of booking referees for tournaments and league matches with an automated scheduling, payment, and dispute resolution system.

Analogous to how 'OpenTable' automated restaurant reservations and 'Mindbody' automated fitness class bookings, padel clubs (a rapidly growing sport) rely on WhatsApp groups and spreadsheets to find and pay referees for matches. This is a friction point for club managers who waste hours coordinating, and for referees who want a reliable way to find paid gigs. The solo operator builds a simple booking engine with a referee directory, automated scheduling, payment processing (taking a % cut), and integrated ratings. Distribution is owned via direct outreach to the top 100 padel clubs in a single country (e.g., Spain, Argentina) using a free trial.
