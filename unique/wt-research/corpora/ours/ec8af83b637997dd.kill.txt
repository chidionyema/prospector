Clinical Trial Caregiver Match.

A transaction broker that matches a carer’s dependent (dementia, Parkinson’s, rare disease) with actively enrolling clinical trials, negotiating a per‑enrolment payment from the trial sponsor and handling all paperwork—turning the carer into a supplier of eligible subjects instead of a passive seeker.

Carers exhaust hours searching ClinicalTrials.gov and cold‑calling sites, often missing trials with compensation or cutting‑edge care. By inverting the dynamic—the broker actively ‘sells’ the patient’s profile to trial sponsors who pay for recruitment—the carer gains access to otherwise invisible trials and the broker earns a fee from the CRO, supplementing the carer’s constrained budget.

Clinical research organisations (CROs) / trial sponsors pay a finder’s fee per qualified enrolled patient; the carer may pay a small upfront vetting fee to ensure commitment.

Decentralised trial models are expanding, creating a shortage of diverse, community‑based participants; at the same time, an ageing population yields more in‑home carers desperate for no‑cost treatment alternatives.
