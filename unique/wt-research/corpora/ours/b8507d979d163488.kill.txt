UC Taper Trap.

A flat‑fee, done‑for‑you report service that shows a Gen Z gig worker on Universal Credit exactly how many hours to work to maximise their disposable income, by reverse‑engineering the benefit taper with a proprietary net‑income simulator.

lens: invert (the government publishes rules but structurally cannot give advice on how to legally game them). Friction: a Gen Z worker on Universal Credit sees their earnings wiped out by the 55% taper and work allowance rules, yet has no tool to calculate the optimum work pattern that balances UC, tax, and net pay. Change: the taper rate reduction to 55% (2021) and the expansion of the Administrative Earnings Threshold create a more complex incentive landscape. The service inverts the government’s free “benefit calculator” by modelling multiple what‑if scenarios and delivering a personalised recommendation sheet that a regulated benefits agency could never publish because it would look like incitement to reduce work effort.

Gen Z gig workers or freelancers who receive Universal Credit and have some irregular monthly income (e.g., £400‑£1,200/month).

With the post‑pandemic restart, many Gen Zers are mixing part‑time gig work with UC for the first time; ignorance of the taper is costing them hundreds of pounds annually, and the DWP’s own calculators only show current entitlement, not forward‑looking optimisation.
