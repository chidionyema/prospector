DutyDrawBack – The SME Import Brokerage Fee Reclaimer.

A done-with-you service for small product businesses that audits their courier/delivery import duty invoices, identifies hidden broker fees and overcharges, and semi-automates the reclaim process by generating structured dispute letters that cite carrier tariff regulations.

Analogous to the chargeback defense engines, but for cross-border trade: small businesses importing goods are often charged inflated 'disbursement fees' by express carriers under the guise of customs handling, which are actually regulated and challengeable. A solo can build a rule-engine that flags excessive fees based on carrier published tariffs and helps reclaim them.
