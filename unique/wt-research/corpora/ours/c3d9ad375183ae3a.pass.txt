AI safety audits for Georgia poultry line workers.

An AI-camera service that audits poultry line workers' ergonomic safety against OSHA recordable criteria, so plants catch injuries before they're filed.

Georgia is the largest poultry-producing state, with plants clustered around Gainesville (the self-styled Poultry Capital of the World), Canton, and Claxton. Poultry processing has the highest injury rate of any major industry, and the Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes annual injury data showing the gap. Many Georgia poultry plants already run line cameras for quality control; those same camera feeds can flag OSHA-recordable ergonomic events (repetitive debone motion, blade proximity, slip patterns, cold-stress recovery) that the human safety inspector catches only on a daily walk-around.

The service ingests existing line-camera feeds, runs the AI model, and delivers a weekly safety dossier to the plant manager. Pricing is per line per shift, with monthly bundles for multi-plant operators.

This borrows from how professional sports teams use AI-camera analytics (NFL Next Gen Stats, NBA player tracking) to flag injury risks before they happen, applying that pattern-recognition discipline to industrial lines. The model is trained on poultry-line footage, a niche dataset a generic factory-floor safety vendor never collects, and plants also won't share line footage with a generic vendor who competes with their integrator customers.
