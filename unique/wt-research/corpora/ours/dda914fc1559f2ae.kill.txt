The Zero-Cost Lease Renewal Trigger.

A PLG tool that auto-generates and files a rent review or lease renewal notice for commercial tenants in the UK's rising-rent market, taking a 20% cut of the rent reduction or deferral it secures.

Small business tenants (e.g., independent retailers, cafes, co-working spaces) with rent reviews or lease breaks coming up in a rising-rate environment are individually too busy and intimidated by legal process to challenge their landlord's proposed rent increase. By making a single free input (property address + lease end date) and using Land Registry data and statute-mandated forms (e.g., s.25 notice, third-party rent assessment application), the tool generates a legally compliant trigger document the tenant can file themselves. The tool only charges if the tenant achieves a rent reduction or deferral (via a linked solicitor panel that takes a success fee). The wedge: the tenant's own inertia and the fact that no free tool currently automates the specific statutory notice needed to force a rent review or break.
