The Solo Urban 'Gig Rack' Equipment Audit & Recovery Service.

A solo operator in a large city offers a fixed-fee service to audit and recover 'lost' or under-utilised rooftop cargo racks from parking garages and apartment buildings where residents abandon them after a trip, then cleans, refurbishes, and resells them via a local consignment network.

Urban residents often install rooftop cargo boxes for a road trip, then find them too heavy to bring up to their unit, so they abandon them in the garage. Building management either tolerates it or fines residents. A solo operator can develop a relationship with a few dense urban apartment buildings, offer a free 'rack audit' to the building manager, then contact the owner with a purchase offer for the abandoned rack, then clean, inspect, and list the rack on a dedicated local consignment social media account.

Initially, the building manager pays a small 'audit fee' for a quarterly inspection and removal of abandoned racks. The real payer is the new resident who buys the refurbished rack via consignment (the solo keeps 70% of sale price).
