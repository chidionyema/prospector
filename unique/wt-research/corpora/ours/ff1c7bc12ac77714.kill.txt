RespiteSwap – The Carer-to-Carer Pre‑Booked Slot Exchange.

A text-based service that lets primary carers buy and sell unused respite care slots (day centre places, private care hours) directly with each other, with the broker holding payments in escrow and taking a 12% cut.

Carers routinely lose money when they cancel pre‑paid care slots (illness, appointments) because providers don’t offer refunds. A secondary market where verified carers can trade these slots turns wasted capacity into a liquid asset, while the broker earns a fee on each matched transfer—all run from a phone number via WhatsApp.

Both parties: the seller receives the buyer’s payment minus a 12% broker fee; the buyer pays slightly more than the seller’s asking price (the spread is the broker’s margin).

The normalisation of micro‑communities on WhatsApp/Telegram for sensitive exchanges (parent groups, patient forums) provides a trust‑based infrastructure that didn’t exist five years ago; AI can now auto‑match availability, vet users, and handle escrow with a simple Stripe flow, making a solo broker feasible.
