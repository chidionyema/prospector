ShiftCV – the gig worker's career switch kit.

We turn your delivery shifts into a CV that gets interviews, then apply to real UK jobs for you—one fixed fee.

Many gig workers on zero‑hours platforms want stable employment but feel trapped: their fragmented work history looks thin on paper, and they lack the time to craft applications that connect with traditional employers. ShiftCV is a productised service that takes a worker’s raw gig data (mileage, customer ratings, time‑management evidence) and reframes it into the language of reliability and initiative that UK hiring managers look for. We build a tailored CV, a LinkedIn profile snippet, and three employer‑ready cover letters, then search indeed.co.uk and reed.co.uk for matching roles and submit the applications on the client’s behalf. The service costs £249 and includes a follow‑up call to coach them on the first interview.

The model borrows the way boutique CV‑writing shops work, but productises it for a mass‑market Gen‑Z audience, using automation and a distributed team of career consultants to keep costs down. 

It holds because the personal data mapping from gig metrics to employer‑friendly language builds a dataset of what phrasing works, creating a lightweight proprietary advantage over generic CV services.
