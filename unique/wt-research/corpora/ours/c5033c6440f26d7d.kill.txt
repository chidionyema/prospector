WattRefund — the Texas small business tool that finds overcharges on your electricity bill and gets your money back.

A web app that reads your business’s electricity bills, spots billing errors, and disputes them automatically — you pay only if you get a refund.

Small businesses in Texas’s deregulated electricity market often pay more than they should because bills are complex and errors go unnoticed. This tool borrows the consumer bill‑negotiation model (think Billshark) and applies it to commercial electricity — but instead of negotiating rates, it uncovers specific billing mistakes that entitle the holder to a refund under Texas Public Utility Commission rules. You upload a PDF bill; the system parses the line items, checks them against a database of tariff structures and common error patterns (misapplied demand charges, incorrect meter multipliers, phantom fees), then generates a formal dispute letter to the retail electric provider. It takes a 25% contingency fee on any refund obtained — no upfront cost. Because the tool is trained on thousands of corrected bills, its error library compounds, making each new case faster and more precise. A free alternative is calling the provider yourself, but most small‑business owners lack the time, regulatory knowledge, or confidence to fight a utility. Energy brokers focus on procurement, not post‑sale error recovery, and a utility structurally cannot offer a tool that challenges its own billing.
