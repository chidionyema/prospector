PitchGauge — The Street-Food Trader's Event Bid Ceiling Engine.

A vertical tool that connects to a food truck's Square/SumUp till and turns real takings history plus a compounding cross-trader event-revenue dataset into a go/no-go verdict and maximum pitch-fee bid for every market, festival and pitch invitation they receive.

Lens: narrow — drilled from 'hospitality analytics' to the single decision that makes or breaks a street-food trader's year: which events to book and what pitch fee to accept. A trader running 1-3 units commits £150-£3,000 per event in pitch fees, staffing and stock weeks in advance with zero verified revenue data — organizers only ever quote footfall hype. PitchGauge ingests the trader's own till exports (Square/SumUp/Zettle APIs), tags each trading day to a named event, and benchmarks it against anonymised takings from every other subscribing trader who worked the same event. Each customer's data makes the event-level revenue benchmark deeper, producing a bid-ceiling calculator ('at £850 pitch fee, Event X breaks even at your P25 takings — decline or counter at £520') that no organizer or POS vendor will ever publish. Built and run by one person: till APIs for ingestion, an event-matching layer, and a per-event verdict report; distribution is free via NCASS forums, KERB trader groups and street-food Facebook/WhatsApp communities where pitch-fee horror stories are the daily topic.
