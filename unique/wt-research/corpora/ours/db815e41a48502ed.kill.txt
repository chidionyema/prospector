The Creative's Almanac — your seasonal guide to exactly when to pitch fashion, education, retail and charity projects, so you catch the budget before it’s spent.

A quarterly digital magazine that predicts the best months to pitch creative projects to different UK sectors, based on historical spending cycles and events.

Borrowing from the farmer’s almanac, which predicts planting and harvest windows, this media product applies the same concept to the gig economy. Freelance creatives often pitch at the wrong time, missing the window when clients are allocating budgets. By analyzing public data like quarterly corporate earnings calls, ad-spend reports, grant cycles, funding rounds, and cultural events (e.g. London Fashion Week, Christmas campaigns), the almanac builds a predictive model of when each creative niche (fashion photography, e-learning design, packaging, etc.) sees a spike in demand. Each quarter, subscribers receive a digital magazine with visual timelines, lead-time warnings, and actionable pitch guides for the next six months. The wedge is proprietary_data: the amalgamated dataset and the forecasting model, refined year on year, become increasingly accurate and hard to replicate. A free blog post cannot match the depth or timeliness. The solo operator automates much of the data collection via APIs and manual curation, but the editorial analysis is the unique selling point.
